HYBRID — Today screen, first-run state — round two

The greeting zone only: 8 fresh concepts

New constraint: the three chooser cards are frozen — every mock below renders them pixel-identical to what ships today. All eight ideas work exclusively in the strip above them: the greeting, the date, the question, and “FREE”. The seam to heal is the same one — two near-equal headlines and a meta label with no home.

01 — FLIP THE HIERARCHY

The Inversion

Round one demoted the question; this does the opposite. The cards below answer “How do you want to start?” — so that question is the operative line and earns the headline size. The greeting becomes the warm mono kicker above it, and the date + FREE share one quiet meta line beneath. One headline, and it’s the one the screen is actually about.

The Ive moveRank by function, not sentiment. The line that drives the next tap gets the weight; the pleasantry gets the whisper.

02 — THE APP STORE MOVE

Today

Apple’s calmest screen — the App Store “Today” tab — solved this exact problem: caption date on top, one enormous noun, nothing else. Here the headline is simply Today, the date and FREE share the caption line above it, and the greeting survives as one small warm sentence beneath. The question is gone; three cards under the word “Today” need no introduction.

The Ive moveName the moment, not the mechanism. “Today” is what the screen is; “how do you want to start” is plumbing.

03 — A TRAINING JOURNAL COVER

Masthead

The date stops being a mumbled mono caption and becomes the composition: a large serif 19 with the month stacked beside it behind a hairline, the greeting riding shotgun. It reads like the cover of today’s training journal — which is literally what the Today screen is. The question drops to a mono kicker above the cards, finally the same voice and size as “FREE” across from it.

The Ive moveFind the one honest ornament. A date is real content that changes every day — promote what’s true instead of decorating what isn’t.

04 — BRAUN ON THE FACEPLATE

Engraved

Every line survives, but the question changes material: from a competing bold headline to a wide-tracked uppercase label — the way Braun engraves a function name on a faceplate. Engraving can’t compete with a headline; it can only label. The greeting (now 26) becomes the page’s undisputed voice, and the engraved question + FREE form one balanced label row above the cards.

The Ive moveChange the material, not the message. Rams never let a label shout; a label’s job is to be found when looked for, and otherwise disappear.

05 — BORROW THE BRAND’S OWN MOTIF

The Rule

The header already contains the answer: the little chartreuse rule under HYBRID. is the brand’s one graphic gesture. Reuse it as the zone divider — the question sits on a fence line that starts with that same lime segment and runs to “FREE” at the far end. The greeting owns the top; the rule row is unmistakably structure, not a second headline, because the page has already taught you what that lime dash means.

The Ive moveA family of gestures. The best new element is one the design already owns — repetition of a single motif is what makes a product feel inevitable.

06 — MAXIMUM COMPRESSION

One Line

The entire zone collapses to a single baseline: greeting left, date right, sharing one line the way a letterhead does. The question is deleted, “FREE” right-aligns quietly above the stack, and the cards rise ~90px closer to the fold — on the real screen that pulls “How ready do you feel?” fully into view. The cheapest concept here in vertical pixels, and the one that most respects the cards as the content.

The Ive moveSpace is a feature. Every line you remove above the fold is given back to the athlete’s actual choices — deference measured in pixels.

07 — BIND, DON’T HEADLINE

The Spine

The real oddness is that the question floats with no visible relationship to the three cards that answer it. So give it one: a thin chartreuse spine runs from the (now small, calm) question down the left of the whole stack, physically binding question to answers — the way a bracket groups options on paper. The cards themselves are untouched; the spine lives in the page margin. The greeting stays the sole headline above.

The Ive moveExpress relationships structurally. If two things belong together, connect them with geometry — don’t caption them and hope.

08 — THE GREETING AS AN EVENT

Settle

Maybe the greeting shouldn’t be furniture at all. On open, it appears alone — centered, generous, the only thing on the glass — and after a breath (~600 ms) it settles: shrinking up into a compact one-line row as the cards rise into place. You get the full warm hello every time you arrive, and a clean, single-headline layout every second after. The static layout that remains is Concept 06; the greeting becomes choreography instead of a permanent second headline.

The Ive moveDesign the transition, not just the state. A greeting is a moment — let it behave like one, then defer. (Reduced-motion users simply get the settled frame.)
