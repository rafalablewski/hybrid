01 · Executive summary
The thesis

A coach-led wedge into the fitness market.

Cold consumer installs convert at 1–2% and churn fast. A platform a coach hands you in person — and uses with you every week — converts an order of magnitude better and retains because the relationship, not the app, is the lock-in. So the entry strategy is B2B2C: land the coach (recurring seat revenue), embed in the gym (free distribution + retention story), and convert two pools of warm users — gym members and coached clients — onto the paid consumer tier. Each (5 gyms + 5 coaches) set is a self-contained, profitable “beachhead unit” that one founder/rep can close in 8 weeks and then replicate.

02 · Value creation
Who benefits, and how

Three beneficiaries. One aligned system.

The model only scales if every party is structurally better off. The member's experience (adaptive sessions, PRs, recaps, accountability) is the engine of retention — but the three parties who decide whether HYBRID wins are the gym, the coach, and us.

For the gym

Retention & differentiation — at zero cost

  • Churn radar: the Accountability Engine flags members going quiet before they cancel — directly defends membership revenue.
  • “Coached gym” positioning: every membership ships with an adaptive app rivals can't match — a sales differentiator.
  • Higher PT attach: the app makes coaching visibly valuable, lifting personal-training sales (the gym's highest-margin line).
  • Zero ops, optional upside: no hardware, no integration; optional revenue share on coach seats sold in their gym.
For the coach

More clients, less admin, recurring online income

  • Scale past floor hours: hold up to 40 athletes on one seat, including remote & travelling clients.
  • Onboard anyone in seconds: add a client by email, SMS or a QR code they scan — even clients who've never heard of the app land already linked to you.
  • Build once, assign to many: author a program, push it to a client or a whole group in two taps.
  • The weekly check-in loop: the ritual that converts one-off plans into monthly recurring client revenue.
  • Clients on the house: your clients use the app free — they get a read-only view of the plan & diet you assigned, so there's no per-client cost to you.
For us (the company)

Recurring revenue, near-zero CAC, a data moat

  • Predictable B2B MRR: coach seats are sticky, expand with roster size, and carry ~96% margin.
  • Two upsell pools: gym members and coached clients self-convert Casual → Pro once hooked — no paid acquisition.
  • Capital-efficient GTM: 1,650 warm users from 10 in-person conversations; the unit is replicable.
  • Compounding data: every logged session & check-in refits the engine — better adaptation → higher retention → cheaper growth.

04 · Revenue integrity
The leakage question, solved by design

A coach link never grants Pro.

The classic worry with any bundled tier is leakage — people fake-joining a coach to dodge the $12.99. We remove the incentive at the root. A coach relationship gives a client the free app plus a read-only view of what's been assigned (plans, programs, diet) — and nothing more. The paid powers (editing, adding, building, the adaptive engine) stay behind the Pro upgrade. There is simply no Full to farm.

Coached clients get the free app + a read-only view of what their coach assigned. Pro is never bundled into a coach seat — so it can't be gamed.
1

Entitlement is independent of the coach link This change

Today, a live coach link silently unlocks the full athlete tier on the coach's seat. We're cutting that path: a client's tier is decided solely by their own billing. An active coach now unlocks read-only assigned content, not paid features.

2

Coached = Casual + read-only assigned This change

A coached client keeps everything Casual allows (log workouts & intake, history, follow plans) and additionally sees the plan, program & diet their coach assigned — view-only. To edit, add or self-program, they upgrade to Pro like anyone else.

3

Relationship integrity still holds Live

Coach is a verified, admin-approved role; clients attach only via mutual-consent, audited links; access ends when the link ends. These protect the coaching relationship — and now there's no entitlement riding on them to exploit.

4

The old leak becomes a lead Upside

Coached clients are warm, engaged, already-in-the-app users. With Pro the only way to self-program, a meaningful share upgrades — turning what would have been a free-rider loophole into a second consumer-revenue pool.

Net effect: joining a coach yields zero paid capability, so the exploit's payoff is $0 — there's nothing to police. Meanwhile every coached client becomes a Pro upsell target. We close the leak and open a revenue line in the same move.

07 · The product
What you actually demo

Not a tracker. An adaptive coaching system.

The five things to show in the gym office — all shipped, web and iPhone.

01

Adaptive prescription

Working loads dosed from the athlete's own lifts and today's recovery (readiness + HPI). The plan moves with the person — the part a spreadsheet can't do. A Pro-tier power.

02

Coach console

Build a plan, program or diet, push it to a client or a whole group at once. Mutual-consent, private coach↔client; the client sees it read-only on the free app.

03

Weekly check-in

The retention heartbeat: client submits metrics + note, coach reviews and replies in-app. The reason clients pay monthly.

04

Come-back loop

PR celebrations, shareable recaps, streaks, the Future-Self simulator, and an Accountability Engine that predicts the quit and nudges.

05

Free plan library

Curated goal-first plans a member can follow free — value before anyone pays, and the top of the consumer funnel.

08 · Execution
The 8-week ground game

Land coaches first, then gyms, then the floor.

Weeks 1–2Coaches

Sign 5 coaches on founding seats

Demo the console live, set up their first program, onboard 2–3 clients in the room. Free 90-day founding seat in exchange for a testimonial + one case study. Start with the people who pay you.

Weeks 2–4Gyms

Walk into 5 gym offices with proof

You're no longer selling an idea — a coach in their own gym is already using it. Pitch retention + “coached gym” positioning, free, with optional seat revenue share. Agree a member launch date.

Weeks 4–6Members

Activate the floor

Staffed table for a week: scan → install → follow a free plan → log session one, same visit. Bring the coaches; members trust them. Capture testimonials while energy is high.

Weeks 6–8Systematize

Measure, package, repeat

Pull installs, 30-day actives, check-in streaks, PRs. Turn the best into a one-page case study — the asset that makes unit #2 a 20-minute close. Turn billing on as trials end.
