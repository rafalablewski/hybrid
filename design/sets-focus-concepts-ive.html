The through-line. None of these adds anything — they all subtract. The screenshot's chaos was outlined boxes and eight equal-weight rows; every concept here removes the boxes and lets one set be seen through scale, space, tone, or a single quiet mark of chartreuse.

Ships this week: 03 Descending tone and 10 Ambient light — same list, just a state→tone/light pass, no relayout. The reduction bets: 02 The one set and 09 Two states — radically less on screen, best for high-volume days. My pick: 01 Quiet ledger — it keeps every set visible (athletes want to see the plan) but calms all of them, and the single chartreuse dot is the most Ive gesture in the set.
