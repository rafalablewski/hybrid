HYBRID — Light theme redesign — Concept 12

Champagne & Graphite

The jewellery-house answer. Champagne silk surfaces, graphite ink, and gold that appears only as hairlines — a gilded top edge on each card, a ruled line under the maison wordmark — never as a surface, never as a button. The action is graphite, so it reads as velvet against silk. Italiana, the thin display face of perfume flacons, is letter-spaced wide and centred; opening the app should feel like opening a jewel case.
