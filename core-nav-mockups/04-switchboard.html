04 — Switchboard

The current bars never show how to get between surfaces — POS gives no hint that KDS or Floor exist. This layout makes the top-level switch the hero: a labelled module switcher on the left (tap to jump anywhere) plus a compact icon rail of the other four surfaces for one-tap moves. Sub-tabs sit right beside it. On the far right, the four loose global pills collapse into one bordered status cluster — ops · venue · clock · alerts — so the whole right edge reads as a single object instead of scattered chips.
