Insight flows from summary → drill-down → horizon.

2PERFORMANCE TWIN (HPI) — the headline
The cockpit's hero is the one number that summarizes everything: a single big HPI with trend, the three sub-scores, and a one-line driver. It's first because a command center should let you read your whole state in two seconds before drilling anywhere.
Pattern: full-width hero card — oversized metric + sparkline + 3-up sub-scores + driver line.

4ATTENTION — injury risk (conditional)
Risk is an exception-driven alert: it earns a high slot only when something is flagged, and disappears (or collapses to a thin "all clear" line) when not. Placing it right after "right now" means a warning is seen before you commit to today's session — but it never out-ranks your positive headline state.
Pattern: conditional alert card with accent border; collapses when clear.

6BREAKDOWN — disciplines (tabbed)
The four discipline deep-dives (Strength, Endurance, Sport, Velocity) were four separate stacked sections — that's a lot of scroll for data most users check occasionally. Collapsing them into one tabbed module keeps drill-down available without making everyone scroll past three sections they didn't open.
Pattern: tabbed module — one card, swipeable discipline tabs, consistent stat grid.

7HORIZON — goal & season setup
Goal/season setup was at the very top of the old cockpit — but it's the least frequently changed thing. Moved to the bottom as a long-horizon timeline + a single "adjust plan" action. The pinned context bar (#1) keeps the *summary* always visible, so demoting the editor costs nothing day-to-day.
Pattern: vertical timeline of phases + one configuration CTA.
NEW TOP-TO-BOTTOM ORDER
  1. Sticky context header (goal/phase/countdown)
  2. Performance Twin / HPI hero
  3. Right now: readiness + today's route
  4. Attention: injury risk (conditional)
  5. This week: recap + PRs
  6. Breakdown: disciplines (tabbed)
  7. Horizon: goal & season timeline
Flow logic: one headline state → what acts on today → exceptions/alerts → retrospective → drill-down → long horizon. Frequently-read rises; rarely-edited setup sinks (but stays summarized in the sticky bar).
Biggest moves: Goal/season top → bottom; 4 stacked discipline sections → 1 tabbed module; injury risk promoted to a conditional alert; HPI promoted to the hero.
