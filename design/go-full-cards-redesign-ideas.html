Ten ways to de-slop the Cockpit & Sport cards

The shipped pair reads as generic AI filler — a gradient-bordered rounded card, monospace everywhere, an ALL-CAPS arrow kicker — and the two CTAs don't line up because Cockpit runs three lines and Sport runs two. Each idea below fixes both: a distinct, intentional treatment, and one shared grid skeleton so the title, body and CTA sit at the exact same position in both widgets. Same copy throughout — only the design changes. Set in the app's real faces: Sohne & Söhne Mono.
