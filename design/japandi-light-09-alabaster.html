HYBRID — Light theme redesign — Concept 09

Alabaster

The museum answer. Every border is removed — cards are carved out of the alabaster ground by two layers of soft light, the way a plinth separates from a gallery floor. Marcellus, the lapidary Roman face of gallery walls, sets titles with quiet authority; metadata is set like exhibit labels. The single material accent is bronze, used the way museums use it: small plaques, a coin for today, never paint.
