HYBRID — TODAY

All done for today. The plan card's finished state.

Today, finishing the scheduled workout barely registers on the home screen: the plan card keeps showing the full prescription with a lone "View in history" ghost button, and the workout you just did never appears in the Done today card below. These concepts close the loop: the card's session area collapses into a calm "All done for today" state that mirrors the rest-day treatment, and the finished workout moves down into Done today as a first-class row.

The guidance (verbatim requirements)
  1. After the scheduled workout is done, the section turns into "All done for today" — visually reflecting the rest-day screen (glyph + title + quiet mono note).
  2. The finished workout moves to the "Done today" card below, as a tappable row.
  3. The "off-plan — beyond the schedule" sub-line is deleted.
