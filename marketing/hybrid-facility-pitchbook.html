HYBRID — Performance platform for gyms & coachesThe platform

The platform — why it's defensible

A decision engine, not a content library.

  • HPI — Hybrid Performance Index. One 0–100 score fusing strength, endurance and recovery, with the limiter named — a shared language for coach and member.
  • Adaptive prescription. Loads adjust to each athlete's readiness (own HRV / RHR / sleep baselines) and accumulated fatigue.
  • Velocity-based training. Capture bar speed, estimate 1RM without max testing, auto-prescribe today's working load.
  • Confidence compounds with data. The more a member logs, the sharper the engine — a moat that deepens the longer they stay.

Built & shipped today

Web + native mobile apps · coach program builder · group assignment · roster & squad monitor · team benchmarking · private notes & check-ins · the full engine, unit-tested.

Rolling out — founders set the order

Full plan library across 19 goals (Bodybuilding live today), wearable sync (WHOOP / Oura / Garmin / Apple), in-app messaging & reminders.
