Roughly ordered by effort: 05 Sunset and 10 Focus-dim are near-zero-risk CSS passes on your current table; 01 Spotlight, 06 Collapse-identical and 08 Ledger restructure the list for real calm; 03 Stepper and 07 Hero ring rethink the interaction entirely.
