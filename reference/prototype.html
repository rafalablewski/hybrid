HYBRID Prototype

The ambient interface as a working app — Today and its three tabs, Sport, Fuel and the live set. Nothing here is a picture. Log a set and watch readiness fall, tomorrow's bar drop, your protein target rise and HPI's limiter change, because the same simplified engines feed all four screens. That interconnection is the product; the screens are just where it surfaces.

Try this, in order

Each step changes numbers on the other three screens. The point is not the screen you are on — it is what moves everywhere else.

Change the morning

The prescription is computed from sleep and resting heart rate, not hard-coded. Flip the night and the coach changes its mind — same engine, opposite instruction.

What is actually simulated

Readiness falls out of sleep debt, resting-HR delta and accumulated muscle cost. The prescription is that morning readiness mapped to a load factor and rounded to the plate. Freshness decays per working set and takes a 9-point hit from a run. The protein target scales with the tonnage you actually move, so finishing your session raises it while you stand there. HPI is the three pillars weighted, which is why a hard session pushes strength up and recovery down and the headline barely moves — an honest result, and one a rings-and-streaks app would never show you.

Real HYBRID does all of this properly in readiness.ts, effort.ts, recovery-pairs.ts, landmark-adapt.ts and hpi.ts. These are toy versions with the same shape.

What is faked, honestly

Voice is a menu, not a microphone — tap the ⏺ button and pick an utterance. The run is a three-second animation rather than 46 minutes. The film is real motion graphics but the narration is text, not audio. Nothing here needs a camera, which is the finding rather than a limitation.
