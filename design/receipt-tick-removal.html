Design study – Today / Your training

The tick, the gutter, and the exit hanging off it

Three changes to the finished-day card, and only one of them is the tick. Removing the ✓ collapses the 31px column it sat in, which is what actually moves the card: every line now starts on the card's own content edge, so there is one left edge instead of two. The View in history exit was indented to that column. Looking for it a home is what exposed the third change: it doesn't need one. The Today screen already carries a door into History, so the card's link was the second one — and the only one that couldn't fit.

What was actually wrong

Measured, not felt
3× one fact

The day chip is marked trained, the headline reads Logged, and the figures are a receipt for work that plainly happened. The ✓ was the loudest of the three and the only one carrying no information.

31px dead column

The gutter existed to hold the glyph, so the receipt sat at 51 while the seam, the session row and the pill beneath it sat at 20. The card alternated between two edges, and drew nothing at either to say which one it meant.

1stray accent

Chartreuse is the app's act now colour. On a finished day the only thing left to do is log another sport, and the ✓ was spending the accent on a state instead.

2rails, 2 exits

The same six words, drawn twice: 18px of margin in one rail, a hairline and 16px of padding in the other, above its catch-up list. Both indented 31px, and both pointing at a screen this page already has a labelled door to.

Chartreuse = the card's content edge (20px). Terracotta = the legacy 31px indent, which nothing lands on after this change.

Where the exit goes

Three homes, none of them right

The house rule is explicit on the form: an exit is never a bordered box, and a ring means it leaves. It is quieter on the place — an exit lives at the end of the thing it exits. All three of these obey the form. They differ on what "the end" means, and on whether the two rails can wear the same answer: the plan rail's header slot is already occupied by DAY 3 / 84, and its finished day offers no actions at all.

None of the three shipped. B was built, and it collided — which sent us back to the question all three were answering. The Today screen already carries a door into History: a labelled door row after the retrospective clusters, placed by the rule that there is one exit point after all the breakdowns. The day card's link was a second door to the same room, 400dp above the first, in a different vocabulary, and it was the one that could not fit. So the card carries no exit at all now — it states the day and offers the next action, and leaving is the screen's job. See design/done-card-final.html for the card as it ships.

And the enrolled rail, which had it worse

Plan week rail – a finished plan day

The two rails are the same object in two modes — enrolling changes the card's fill, never its shape — so the exit could not be fixed on one and left on the other. In plan mode it was worse: a second hairline in a card whose whole separation budget is one, and it sat above the catch-up list, offering a way out with sessions still to read underneath.

Held against the house rules

CLAUDE.md
✓

An exit is never a bordered box

No fill, no border, no radius, no shadow — and no ring, because a ring belongs to a rail tail or a door row, not to a quiet label at a card's foot.

✓

Use the shared component, never a local copy

Two hand-rolled exits with two different arrangements became one. That drift was the whole reason the rule exists.

✓

The two rails wear one separation device

The plan rail's second hairline is gone. One card, one fill, one rule — enforced by a HARD test.

✓

No decorative marker before a label

The ✓ was a status glyph rather than decoration, which is why it was allowed — but it stated a fact the headline and the chip already carried.

✓

Never a middot as a separator

The receipt's figures are separated by a flex gap; the session row's meta line uses a spaced en dash.

✓

Keep the capabilities registry current

Updated in the same change, with the reasoning, not just the outcome.

What deliberately did not change

Scope

The week strip, the load bars and today's chartreuse disc are untouched. The receipt still picks one hero figure by the same priority the History rows use. The seam still opens the day's sheet, and it keeps its own arrow — that arrow and the History exit point at two different places (this day's sessions, and every day), which is defensible, but two outward arrows within 60px of each other is the next thing worth looking at. Say so and it becomes its own study.
