The idea. Where the last batch reduced with flat type, this one reduces with material — glass, depth, and light lift the active set instead of a coloured box. Every concept keeps the same data; they differ in how physically the next set separates from the rest.

Most on-brand: 01 Liquid Glass — it literally advances your Aurora × SwiftUI Liquid Glass roadmap item. Most reachable-focus: 02 Live Activity — pins the current set no matter how long the list. Safest: 06 Grouped list — a native iOS screen your users already know.
