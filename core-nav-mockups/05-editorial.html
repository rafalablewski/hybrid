CORE OS · Top Nav Study

05 — Editorial

Confidence over density. The current surface is stated as a large 900-weight wordmark — you always know where you are at a glance across the room — with sub-tabs as quiet mono underline links beneath it. Everything else is dialled to a whisper: icon-only tools with no borders, ops & venue rendered as small labelled metrics (not pills), a single big clock, and one high-contrast white primary button so the accent orange is reserved purely for state (active tab, at-risk, live). Maximum calm, maximum legibility on a busy floor.
