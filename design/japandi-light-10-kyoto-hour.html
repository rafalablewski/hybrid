Kyoto Hour

The true-Japandi answer — the Japan half of the word finally speaks. Warm washi ivory, sumi-ink text, a deep pine action colour, and rules drawn thin and doubled like tatami edging. Shippori Mincho, a Japanese book face, gives the Latin titles a brushed vertical grace no Western serif has. The single flourish is a hanko: the app's small vermilion seal, stamped slightly askew beside Today — a mark, never a message.
