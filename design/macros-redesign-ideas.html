Macros, six ways

Today Macros mode is four identical boxes. These give it its own language — colour, split, and target-awareness. Fixed palette: protein lime, carbs teal, fat sand. The Calories tab stays exactly as-is; every phone here is shown on the Macros tab. All fields are live.

Enter it the way you think
