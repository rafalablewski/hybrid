Aurora · Profile R&D

The profile, pushed to 10/10 — 5 directions

Revised to your notes. HPI is private — it's your own performance indicator, shown only to you and never on any public-facing surface. There are no Edit / Share buttons; you edit from the edit icon next to your avatar / name. Hybrid membership stays exactly as it is (the FREE pill is untouched). The job is still to fix the dead space and make streak & consistency visual (heatmaps, rings, sparklines) — while keeping the Aurora lime + mono-ID identity.
