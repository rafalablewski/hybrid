Why there is no exit on the card

The measure, then the reason

The exit rode the card's last baseline for exactly one revision, and it collided. The measure says it always would have: a 375pt screen, less the shell's two 12dp gutters and the card's two 20dp paddings, leaves 311dp of content. The pill and the label want more than that between them, and a 320pt device is 46dp worse again. Flex wrap means it never overlapped — it did something quieter and worse: it dropped to a line of its own, which is the orphan the arrangement existed to remove.

On one lineWidth

"VIEW IN HISTORY →"≈133dp17 characters, 11dp mono, 1.2 tracking, uppercase
Required≈329dpAvailable: 311dp. Short by 18 — and by 64 on a 320pt phone.

Shrinking the label, wrapping it, or giving it a full-width row of its own would each have answered the collision. None of them answers the question the collision was asking, which is why the card has an exit at all. This screen already has one, and it is a proper door — glyph, name and subtitle, in the shared door-row anatomy, placed deliberately after the retrospective clusters because the rule is one exit point after all the breakdowns:
