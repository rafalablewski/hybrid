Roles & access

Three roles, each scoped. Access is enforced server-side by relationship, not role label alone.
Client
Owns their own data. Sees only themselves. Private coach notes stay hidden.
Coach
Sees only athletes who accepted them (mutual consent). Can leave private notes. Also a client.
Admin
Platform aggregates & content. No silent access to private training data; support access is audited.
