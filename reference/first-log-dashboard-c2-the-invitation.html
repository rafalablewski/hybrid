The Invitation — empty days are the pitch

Marketing never says a word. After the first log the shipped plan-card geometry appears, and its future days render as dashed, open slots. Tapping tomorrow doesn't show an error or an empty screen — it shows an honest state: "Monday is open." One primary Start-style pill fills the week with a plan; the two circular side buttons — the same action grammar as the plan state's skip/postpone circles — hold "Build" and "Just train". The three onboarding cards disappear entirely after the first log. State C is the shipped plan screen, verbatim: the dashed circle the user tapped becomes a solid prescribed day.

The design argument

The most persuasive advertisement for a plan is the absence it fills. A dashed circle around Monday says more than a card ever could: here is a day, it belongs to you, it is currently silent. We let the void do the selling — quietly, in sand, never in guilt.
