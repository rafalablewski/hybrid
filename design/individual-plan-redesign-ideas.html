HYBRID — Plan page redesign

The plan is a record.
Give it a cover.

Today the individual plan page opens with a rounded gradient card floating inside the screen gutter — a card about the plan, sitting on the page. The Explore tab already solved this identity: the plan cover — duotone discipline wash over near-black, an oversized ghost glyph, a white discipline chip, the title anchored heavy at the bottom. When you tap that cover, the detail page should feel like the cover became the screen, not like you navigated to a different product.

This brief redesigns the whole plan detail: a full-bleed cover hero at the very top that contracts into a sticky glass bar as you scroll, and beneath it the maxes, the week selector and the day tables rebuilt in the app's current material vocabulary — hairlines instead of boxes, one accent per cluster, data set in mono with tabular figures, everything full-bleed where it belongs.

Ask 1 — full width
The hero becomes an edge-to-edge cover — no gutter, no rounded corners, no card frame.
Ask 2 — at the very top
It starts at y = 0, running under the status bar; the safe-area inset becomes the cover's own padding.
Ask 3 — match Explore
One cover recipe, shared: the Explore PlanCover grows up into the detail hero. Same wash, chip, glyph, title.
Ask 4 — redesign the body
Maxes become a ledger, weeks a waveform rail, day tables a quieter programme card.
Ask 5 — sticky contraction
The cover pins and compresses with scroll into a 56 dp glass bar — riding the shipped scroll-collapse signal.

The cover pins, then compresses 0 → 150 dp

The hero is sticky at y = 0 and its height collapses from 300 dp to an 88 dp bar (56 dp + safe area) over the first 150 dp of scroll. It never leaves; the page scrolls underneath it. This is direct manipulation — the cover tracks your finger, frame for frame.

Two titles cross-fade, no FLIP math type

The big bottom-anchored 900-weight title fades out in the first half of the range; a compact 16 pt centred title fades in over the last third. Chip and meta line leave first — hierarchy sheds from the outside in, the name is the last thing standing.

The wash stays, the bar earns a hairline material

The duotone discipline wash never disappears — collapsed, the bar is still unmistakably this plan's bar. It gains a bottom hairline and (on device) a Liquid Glass backdrop blur so content sliding beneath reads as depth, not overlap.

The week rail docks beneath it second stick

The schedule rail is sticky at top: collapsed-height. Once the cover is compact, week switching is always one thumb-reach away while you read Day 4 — the two sticky layers form the screen's permanent chrome.

The CTA surfaces when the cover settles enroll

The enroll pill docks above the tab bar as the cover finishes collapsing — commitment appears once you've actually looked at the work. One CTA on screen, never two.

Every layer below already exists in Explore's PlanCover (web and mobile). The redesign promotes that exact recipe to the detail screen at full size — which is also what makes the shipped shared-element zoom finally usable: the Explore cover and this hero become the same object at two scales.

01 — The hem
Stat columns stay editorial — and sit directly on the ink

The rule-topped stat columns are already the strongest thing on the current page — they stay, verbatim: 2 dp chalk-18% rules, 900-weight tabular numerals, mono nano labels. What changes is their ground: they sit directly on the screen ink, flush under the cover with the standard 16 dp gutter, followed by the blurb. No card. They are the first content to slide beneath the pinned cover, which is exactly the depth cue the collapse needs in its first frames.

02 — The ledger
Maxes stop being a form and become a ledger

Today: a boxed card holding five boxed inputs — a form. Redesigned: a SectionHead ("Your maxes" / mono "KG — optional" on the right, stating the unit once) over hairline rows. Each row is lift name left, a bare underline numeric field right — tabular, right-aligned, no box-in-box.

  • Live echo. While a field is focused, a mono echo shows the first working weight it unlocks — → 59 kg @ 60%. Type a max and the matrix below morphs from percentages to kilograms in place.
  • Prefill from the logbook. A ghost pill offers "Use my logged 1RMs" — the history engine already knows these numbers; empty fields shouldn't.
  • Values keep persisting on-device via the existing shared maxes store — no behavioural change, only material.

03 — The waveform
Week pills become the plan's volume waveform

Eight wrapping pills tell you the plan is eight weeks long; they tell you nothing about its shape. The rail replaces them: one full-bleed horizontal row (the exercise-widget bleed idiom — cards slide under the physical screen edge), each week a slim column whose bar height is that week's real volume from the program data. The Soviet wave and the week-8 taper become visible structure, not decoration.

  • Selection is the accent — the chosen week's bar and label turn lime; everything else stays 16% white. One accent per cluster.
  • It docks. Sticky beneath the collapsed cover, so week switching stays reachable at any scroll depth.
  • Enrolled state: the rail auto-centres the current week; completed weeks dim. "656 lifts" moves to the SectionHead's right meta, where counts live everywhere else in the app.

04 — The programme card
The day table gets quieter and says more

The structure of the current matrix is right — exercises down, loads across, reps in the cells. The redesign changes its voice, not its grammar:

  • Day header in the display face. "Day 1" set 800/16 in chalk — a title, not a mono kicker — with the lift count as right-side mono meta, SectionHead-style. The amber all-caps header retires.
  • Session bands stay semantic. AM keeps the sand tint, PM the blue — a 5% wash plus tinted mono label. That's load semantics, not decoration.
  • The column IS the intensity. Each %-column keeps one tier colour (blue → lime → sand → red as load climbs) for header and cells; the tier system already shipped in the load-colour tokens.
  • Empty cells go silent. The faint placeholder glyph is replaced by nothing — a 12%-white void. An empty cell is information; it doesn't need a character.
  • Sticky exercise column. When the matrix scrolls horizontally, names hold at the left edge so a wide ramp never divorces reps from their lift.
  • Radius parity fix: mobile day cards sit at radius 16 today while web Aurora cards are 28 — both move to the shared card radius.

05 — The dock
One CTA, and it arrives when you've seen the work

The enroll button leaves the page flow and becomes a docked Liquid Glass pill above the tab bar, surfacing as the cover finishes collapsing (p ≥ .45). At the top of the page you're reading a cover; once you're inside the programme, the commitment is always in reach — and there is never an inline button and a docked one on screen at once.

Enrolled, the dock flips to a quiet status pill — ✓ Enrolled — Week 3 of 8 — and tapping it jumps to the current week. If enrolled, a 2 dp progress hairline also runs along the collapsed cover's bottom edge: semantic, not ornamental. The leave-plan section stays where it is, at the very bottom.

Grounded in what's already shipped

No new machinery invented

The scroll signal exists on both clients

  • Web: lib/use-scroll-collapse.ts publishes a registered --scroll-collapse CSS property every frame — zero React re-renders. The hero adds a sibling --hero-collapse over its own 0→150 range; every interpolation in the spec table is a CSS calc()/clamp() off that one number.
  • Mobile: the NavScrollProvider pattern is the same signal — the plan screen drives a Reanimated shared value from the scroll offset and interpolates height, opacity and translate on the UI thread.
  • The nav pill already compresses on this signal; the cover compressing in step with it makes the whole screen breathe as one surface.

This closes a known wave-3 gap

  • The transitions backlog notes the shared-element zoom mechanism (SHARED_ELEMENTS) is shipped and generic but "needs a per-plan detail screen to point at". This hero is that screen: Explore cover → plan cover is the exact zoom pair, same recipe at two scales.
  • Reduce Motion stance is inherited from the shipped masthead compression: scroll-tracking is direct manipulation and stays; only the snap settle degrades.
  • The snap haptic is a true detent — consistent with the audited haptic policy, gated by prefs.haptics.

House rules, kept by construction

  • Full-bleed rule: the cover and the week rail both run under the physical screen edge (negative-gutter idiom from the exercise-widget rail / CoachRail bleed).
  • No middots: cover meta joins with spaced en dashes; matrix empty cells become silence, not a glyph.
  • No decorative dots: every section header is the Explore SectionHead — display-face title left, mono meta right. Section colour appears only in semantic bands (AM/PM, load tiers, RPE heat).
  • Reps stay single numbers — content untouched; this is a material redesign, prescriptions render exactly as authored.

Build map — parity in one change

  • packages/core: extend planHeroView() with the cover recipe — accent, glyph, chip, duration, meta parts — so PlanCover (Explore) and PlanHero (detail) render from one view model and cannot drift.
  • Mobile: plan-hero.tsx becomes the cover + collapse (screen goes edge-to-edge; safe-area inset becomes cover padding); percent-program.tsx gets ledger, rail, programme card; aurora/plans.tsx passes the scroll signal.
  • Web: aurora/plans.tsx PlanHero + program-days.tsx get the same treatment via position: sticky + the CSS custom-property track; full-bleed via margin: 0 calc(-1 * var(--page-pad-x)).
  • Classic (non-program) plan details reuse the identical cover and body vocabulary — one system for all six programs and the goal-tree plans.
  • Accessibility: the compact bar title is the same heading element (no landmark churn); the matrix stays a real table on web with column scope; inputs keep visible labels; Dynamic Type scales the bar height off font metrics.
