01
The wedge

Trainers are the door. Members are the room.

A cold app install converts at a percent or two. A tool your trainer hands you in person — and uses with you every week — converts at a different order of magnitude. So we don't sell HYBRID to 1,650 strangers. We sell it to 10 people (5 owners, 5 trainers) and let them carry it to the floor.

🏋️

The gym owner's pain

Members drift, ghost, and churn. The gym has zero visibility into who's about to quit and no scalable way to make membership feel coached — only the front desk and a hope.

🧑‍🏫

The trainer's pain

Programming in spreadsheets and texts. Capped at the clients they can hold in their head. Check-ins eat their evenings, and clients who travel or train solo fall off.

📱

The member's pain

Generic plans that ignore how recovered they are today. No feedback loop, no sense of progress, nothing that makes them want to come back tomorrow.

02
Three sides, one product

What each side actually walks away with.

Every pitch lives or dies on one question: “what's in it for me?” Here it is, separated out — because the gym owner, the trainer, the client, and you each have a different one.

For the gym

A retention + differentiation layer — for free

  • Churn radar: the Accountability Engine flags members going quiet before they cancel, so staff can reach out with a reason.
  • “Coached gym” positioning: every membership comes with a smart training app — a sales line competitors don't have.
  • Trainer upsell engine: the app makes PT visibly valuable, so more members buy sessions (the gym's margin).
  • Zero ops: no hardware, no integration, no admin. You stick a QR code on the wall and we run the rest.
For the personal trainer

More clients, less admin, recurring online income

  • Build once, assign to many: author a multi-week program, push it to a client or a whole group in two taps.
  • The weekly check-in loop: clients submit, you review & reply in-app — the #1 ritual that makes people pay monthly.
  • Coach remotely: hold 40 athletes on one seat, including the ones who travel or train at home. Income beyond floor hours.
  • Adaptive does the grunt work: loads auto-adjust to each client's recovery, so your plan stays right between sessions.
For their clients & members

A coach in their pocket that actually adapts

  • Today's session, dosed to today's recovery — readiness & the HPI score, not a static PDF.
  • Progress they can see & share: PRs, weekly recaps, streaks, a “future self” projection that rewards showing up.
  • Their trainer, always on: assigned plans, check-in replies, and nudges between sessions.
  • Free to start: log, follow a plan, track history — no card needed to feel the value.
For you (the founder)

Distribution, recurring revenue, and a data flywheel

  • 1,650 warm users from 10 conversations, not a cold ad budget.
  • Recurring B2B revenue: trainers pay monthly seats — predictable MRR that compounds with their retention.
  • Consumer upsell: gym members convert to Pro on their own once they're hooked.
  • Proof + data: real outcomes, testimonials, and the training data that makes the engine smarter — your moat.
03
What HYBRID is

Not a tracking app. An adaptive coaching system.

When you're standing in the gym office, these are the five things to actually show — the features that make a trainer lean in. All shipped, web and iPhone.

01

Adaptive prescription — the engine

Every session's working loads are dosed from the athlete's own logged lifts and how recovered they are today (readiness + the 0–100 Hybrid Performance Index). The plan moves with the person — that's the part a spreadsheet can't do.

02

The coach console — assign, group, program

A trainer builds a plan or a full multi-week program and pushes it straight into a client's account — or to a whole client group at once. Mutual-consent linking, private coach↔client.

03

The weekly check-in — the retention heartbeat

Bodyweight, sleep, soreness, mood, adherence, a note — client submits, trainer reviews and replies in-app. This is the recurring ritual that turns a one-off plan into a monthly subscription.

04

The come-back loop — for the members

PR celebrations, shareable weekly recaps, forgiving streaks, the Future-Self simulator, and an Accountability Engine that predicts the quit and fires the right nudge. Built to pull people back tomorrow.

05

Goal-first plan library — free to follow

Curated plans across strength, physique, endurance, Hyrox/CrossFit, tactical and health. A member can browse, enroll, and follow one free — so there's value before anyone pays.

06
Execution

The 8-week ground game.

A repeatable playbook. Land trainers first (they're the multiplier), prove it on one cohort, then open the floodgates to the members.

Weeks 1–2Land the trainers

Sign 5 trainers on founding seats

Start with the people who make you money. Demo the coach console live, set up their first program, and onboard 2–3 of their clients with them in the room.

  • 1-on-1 demo focused on the check-in + assign-to-group loop
  • Free 90-day founding seat in exchange for a testimonial + 1 case study
  • Get their first real client logging before you leave
Weeks 2–4Sign the gyms

Walk into 5 gym offices with proof

Now you're not selling an idea — you're showing a trainer in their own gym already using it. Pitch the owner on retention + “coached gym” positioning, free.

  • Lead with the churn radar and zero-ops setup
  • Offer the PT-seat revenue share as the sweetener
  • Agree a member launch date + put up the QR posters
Weeks 4–6Activate the floor

Member launch events

A staffed table at each gym for a week: scan-to-install, free plan enrollment, first session logged on the spot. Bring the trainers — members trust them, not you.

  • QR → install → follow a free plan → log session #1, same visit
  • Small incentive (PR board, raffle, free PT consult) to drive installs
  • Capture quick video testimonials while energy is high
Weeks 6–8Prove & systematize

Measure, package, repeat

Pull the numbers — installs, 30-day actives, check-in streaks, PRs. Turn the best stories into a one-page case study. That deck makes gym #6 a 20-minute close.

  • Weekly check-in with each trainer; remove friction fast
  • Publish 2–3 case studies + before/afters
  • Turn billing on for trainers as the 90-day window closes

In the room

Objection handling.

The five things you'll actually hear, and the one-line answer for each.

What they sayWhat you say
“My clients already have an app / spreadsheet.” “Does it adjust today's loads to how recovered they are, and let them check in with you weekly in two taps? That's the part that keeps them paying you.”
“I don't have time to learn new software.” “I'll set up your first program and onboard two clients with you, today, in 20 minutes. If it's not faster than your spreadsheet, walk away.”
(Gym) “What does this cost us?” “Nothing. Members get it free, you get churn alerts and a coached-gym story. The only money flows when a trainer chooses a paid seat — and we'll share that with you.”
“What if my members don't use it?” “That's exactly what the accountability engine is for — it flags the ones going quiet so you can pull them back. Inactivity is the problem it solves, not a risk.”
“$79 is a lot per month.” “It's free for 90 days, and it holds up to 40 clients online. One retained online client covers it — everything after is margin you didn't have before.”
