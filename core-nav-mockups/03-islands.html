03 — Islands

No bar — a set of floating Liquid-Glass capsules on a transparent track, straight out of the Dynamic-Island / visionOS playbook. Each concern lives on its own island: identity, context, section switch, surface actions, and a right-hand status island that carries ops + venue + clock + alerts. Islands appear and disappear per surface (POS shows a QR island; Guest grows a WhatsApp-live island) so the chrome literally reshapes to the screen. This leans into the repo’s existing glass material.
