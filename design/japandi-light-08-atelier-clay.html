HYBRID — Light theme redesign — Concept 08

Atelier Clay

The evolution, not the revolution — for when the clay identity should survive. The diagnosis of the current theme is that it's wrong about light, not about colour: the #eae3d4 ground is a midtone, so cards can't float and clay reads as mud sitting in shadow. Lift the ground three steps, push cards to warm white, give clay its saturation back, and add a single sand wash for chips and active states. The screen keeps its brand and finds its air.
