WHY THIS CONCEPT

  • One decision per fold: Today = "start this session"; Cockpit = "your index, and the one thing to fix." Everything secondary is collapsed, so there's zero scroll-anxiety and a single obvious tap.
  • Progressive disclosure scales: power users expand rows; casual users never see the depth. The same row component handles recovery, week, social, breakdowns — minimal surface, easy parity.
  • Trade-off: calmest and most focused, but the least "alive" — no feed momentum, fewer glanceable numbers up front. Risks feeling under-powered for data-hungry athletes unless the collapsed rows preview enough.
