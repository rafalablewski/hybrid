Things worth trying

What it is running on

Real rules, real arithmetic. The band is d ≥ km && d ≤ km × 1.05, the delta walks the efforts chronologically, the ladder comes from the catalogue, and the prescription doses off [3,4,4,5] sets × [8,6,5,3] reps × [70,75,80,85]% — the shipped constants.

What is faked: the logged efforts themselves (one fixed set per sport, so the page is the same every time you open it) and the effort-zone split, which approximates the real pace-zone engine. Everything else is the shipped logic.

The one figure, earned

The old page opened on best pace and then printed it twice more — as Pace › Best and as the Bests rail's first card. Switch to BEFORE and ring the repeats to see all three at once. The ladder replaces it with something no other screen knows: a time at a distance.

Typed and measured, never alike

Open the ladder's door and type 21:30 — it beats the measured 22:41, so the rung flips to TYPED and loses its pace, because a typed time has no measured distance behind it. Type 24:00 instead and nothing happens to the rung: the measurement still stands.

The page that configures itself

Squash has no distance, so it has no ladder and no pace chart — its marker is a phrase, and the page says so. Tennis has no strength pool, so there is no door. Boxing has nothing logged at all. None of those are branches in the page; they are the catalogue record deciding.

One thing deliberately missing

The volume bars are plain. Folding each week's easy/steady/hard mix into the bar's own fill is built in principle — paceEffortSplit per bucket — and held back because three densities of chartreuse in a 104dp bar is a daylight question a simulator cannot answer.
