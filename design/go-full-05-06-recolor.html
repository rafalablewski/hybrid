Concepts 05 & 06, in three upsell accents

Violet was the premium/coach accent — distinct from the lime the app already spends on everyday actions. So the replacement should stay distinct from that action system too. Below: Liquid Glass (05) and Duotone (06) shown in the three viable palette accents. Same layout, same aligned skeleton — only the accent changes. My pick is amber: it keeps the upsell feeling premium and separate from the lime CTAs.
