HHYBRID — Explore

Explore, built to scale past 50 plans

Five redesigns of the plan-browsing surface. Every one keeps your brief — three full-width feature cards up top, everything else as smaller scrollable cards below — but each answers the “what happens at 50+ plans” question a different way.

Today the Explore list is a single stacked column. At a dozen plans it’s calm; at fifty it becomes an endless scroll with no hierarchy, no grouping, and no way to find “the running ones.” The fix isn’t a longer list — it’s curation up top + horizontal density below.
Mockups render in HYBRID’s shipped Aurora dark theme using the real tokens — --ink #0c0d0c, --lime #c6f84f, Sohne + SohneMono, 28px card radius, full-bleed rails. The phone screens scroll and the rails swipe horizontally — try them. Nothing here touches the codebase; it’s a preview to pick a direction from.
