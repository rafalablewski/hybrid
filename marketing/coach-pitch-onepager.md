# HYBRID — for coaches & gyms

### Strength & conditioning for hybrid athletes — and the operating system for the coaches who train them.

---

**HYBRID is not another workout app.** It's a decision system that sits underneath your
coaching: it reads each athlete's training, readiness, and fatigue, and tells you — and them —
what to do next, and why. You stay the coach. The engine does the math.

---

## The problem you already know

You run a real coaching business on tools that were never built for it:

- **Spreadsheets** for programming — copy, paste, tweak, repeat, for every client.
- **Generic apps** that hand the same plan to everyone and never adjust for the human in front of you.
- **No single view** of who's overreaching, who's dropping off, who's about to get hurt.
- **Hours of admin** that should be spent on the relationship and the room.

The bigger your roster gets, the worse it gets.

---

## What HYBRID does for you

**Build a program once. Assign it to your whole roster.**
Compose weeks → days → exercises (sets × reps × RPE) in the program builder, then push it to
one client or a whole group in two taps. It lands on each athlete's calendar as dated sessions.

**Every client's loads adapt to *them* — automatically.**
The engine adjusts working loads to each athlete's readiness (built from their own HRV, resting
HR, and sleep baselines) and accumulated fatigue. One plan, individualized for twenty bodies,
without you re-writing anything.

**One screen that tells you who needs you today.**
The squad monitor ranks your roster worst-first by readiness, training-load ratio (ACWR), and
injury flags — and auto-segments into *needs-attention / dormant / new / on-track*. You walk in
already knowing where to spend your attention.

**Benchmark your whole team on any lift.**
Line everyone up on the squat, the snatch, a 5k — see who's progressing and who's stalling, on
a ranked chart, in seconds.

**Keep the relationship close.**
Private coaching notes your client never sees, weekly check-ins they can share with you, and
your replies — all in one place, all auditable.

**Coach from the desk or the gym floor.**
Full web app and native iOS/Android app. Your athletes train on either; you manage on either.

---

## Why it's different

Most apps sell *content*. HYBRID is a real, tested **decision engine**:

- **HPI — Hybrid Performance Index:** one 0–100 score fusing strength, endurance, and recovery,
  with the limiter called out so you know what to fix.
- **Velocity-based training:** capture bar speed, estimate 1RM without max testing, and auto-
  prescribe today's load.
- **Confidence that grows with data:** the more an athlete logs, the more certain the engine's
  prescriptions become. It gets to know your athlete the way you do — and never forgets a session.

Think *Bloomberg terminal for the body*, not another feed of workout videos.
