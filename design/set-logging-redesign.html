HYBRID — Logging redesign 02

The active set, refined

Your five notes, applied to the live logger. Everything runs on the Aurora dark palette, Sohne + SohneMono, and the glass slab — tap the numbers, the presets menu, the ⚡, the RPE chip and the log button; it all works.

  • The ⋯ menu = popular presets. Split into the Add-set control — opens a sheet with 3×3, 5×5, 3×12, 4×8 and a link to the full planner.
  • Special is glyph-only. Just the ⚡ — tapping opens a sheet of special set types (warm-up, drop, AMRAP, to-failure…).
  • kg & reps share one baseline, both equal weight. Tapping a number or its unit focuses the input.
  • The giant plus is gone. Logging is now a proper full-width Log set button with a check — sized like the action it is.
  • RPE is hidden until wanted. A quiet chip in the header; tap it and a compact 6–10 scale slides in, then collapses.
