The stock SwiftUI segmented control goes

On iOS the four sources rendered as Picker + .pickerStyle(.segmented). Three things were wrong with it, all visible in the screenshot. AMeter

The fill travels, on the figure's own timing

The figure rolled and the track beside it jump-cut, so one object moved and its twin blinked — for the same event. Every AMeter in the app now travels to its new share on durations.collapse with the same ease-out RollingNumber uses, so a logged food reads as one thing happening. Timing, not a spring, for the reason RollCell already gives: a spring overshoots, and a bar that overshoots is briefly reporting a number that is not true. It does not animate on mount — a screen whose meters all grow from zero on arrival is animating its own layout, not a change — and Reduce Motion sets the width outright, since a width has no position to cross-dissolve.

Motion SourceLine

The selection rule flies instead of cutting

Selection is this control's entire job, and it was the one thing it never showed happening: the 2dp rule was a per-tab border that vanished under one label and appeared under another. It is now a single indicator on springs.lens — the app's named selection token, the same spring the Today hub's pill flies on — measuring each tab so it lands on the label's true width, since these four labels are deliberately unequal. Reduce Motion places it outright, and haptic.selection marks the commit, which is what the codebase does everywhere else you move through discrete values.

On the meal pages, the circle is this screen's verb

It carried Train — the one thing nobody is doing at 23:08 with Snacks open. On the picker it now carries the magnifier and the word Find; everywhere else it is the dumbbell, untouched. That is not a new idea, it is the contract finally being honoured: auroraNavAction has always resolved the verb per surface (Train everywhere, Add post on the feed) and mobile simply could not follow.

And it does not reopen the spent-slot trade. The recorded decision is that the detached slot is never spent on a cross-app search we do not have. This is not a search destination — it is that screen's verb, in exactly the sense Add post is the feed's — and it resolves from a surface, so it can never become the app-wide default. There is a test holding that line.

Stale blocker capabilities.ts
×2 entries

"The native bar cannot do this" stopped being true, and nobody had checked

Two places in the codebase said the system tab bar could not carry a contextual action: "a native trigger is a ROUTE, not a button, and expo-router's unstable native tabs expose no tabPress intercept to repurpose the press." The first half is still true. The second is not, in the version we have installed: a trigger now takes listeners.tabPress, and a trigger marked disabled still emits that press while skipping navigation. A disabled trigger plus a press listener is exactly a button.

So actions grew a kind. route — the circle goes somewhere, an ordinary trigger. screen — it acts on the surface in front of you, a disabled trigger whose press the surface handles. The route name stays log in every state, because name is how the navigator identifies the trigger: swapping it would rename a route under a mounted navigator rather than change a button.

The press lib/nav-surface.ts

It brings the field back under your thumb

A search button on a screen that already has a search field sounds redundant until you scroll. The field is deliberately in flow — it travels with the decision it belongs to rather than pinning — so twenty rows into Recent there was no way back to it but to scroll up. The press scrolls the picker home, puts the cursor in the field and gives a light haptic. The bottom-right corner is where the thumb already is.

The screen publishes both its surface and its handler through a small module store, cleared on unmount so no screen can leave a stale verb — or a dead ref — behind it in the bar. Deliberately not a context: a provider would have to sit above the tabs layout, and every screen in the app would re-render for one trigger.

What is not verified, and what is still open

  • None of the motion has run on a device. The sandbox has no simulator, so the rolling figure, the travelling fills, the flying rule and the tab-bar press are exercised by typecheck, the test suites and the iOS bundle export — nothing more. The first TestFlight build is where they are actually seen.
  • The one I would check first: whether iOS renders a disabled search-role item at full strength or dims it. If it dims, the circle would read as unavailable, and the fix is to keep the trigger enabled and point its route at a redirect instead.
  • Two hand-rolled meters remain in nutrition, logged rather than swept in: the insights card's macro-balance row (a horizontal label|track|value — a different layout) and the WHO/EFSA label panel (a 3dp reference bar — a different claim).
  • The HIT_TARGET guard only polices the shared primitives. A hand-rolled field anywhere else can sit at 43dp and nothing fails — which is how the picker's field did. Widening that rule is its own sweep.
  • The feed's Add post morph is now a small follow-on, not a platform wall. It needs the Today hub to publish its surface and a compose sheet behind a screen-kind action; the mechanism is already carrying the picker.
