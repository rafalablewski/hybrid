Aurora · "More" tab R&D

The feature hub, reimagined — 5 directions

"More" is where everything that doesn't fit the 4 main tabs lives — 40+ tools across Home, Train, Analyze, Recovery, Social, Teams & Account, many gated behind Full. Today it's a flat list of category rows. These five concepts each solve the same problem differently: make many features discoverable, fast to reach, and make the upgrade obvious — without losing the Aurora lime + mono identity.
