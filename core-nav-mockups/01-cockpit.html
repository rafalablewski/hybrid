01 — Cockpit

The professional standard: one 60px row, split into three fixed zones — identity + context (left), the section switch (center, a single segmented control), and utilities (right, grouped by hairline dividers). Contextual tools sit in their own group, always separated from the global ops/location/clock cluster, so the bar never mixes “what am I doing” with “how is the venue.” One accent (warm orange), used only for the active tab underline, the ops chip, and the primary action.
