The middle button, reimagined — 5 directions for the Train launcher

The Train launcher is the app's single most-tapped surface — the screen behind the lime dumbbell. Today it's one giant lime hero, a locked AI billboard, and a scatter of loosely related cards. Each concept below keeps the brand (chartreuse on near-black, mono labels, Archivo weights) but rethinks hierarchy, density, and how the empty state feels. Every screen is rendered on real tokens.
