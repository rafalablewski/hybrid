# HYBRID — coach & gym marketing kit

First-step marketing collateral for pitching **HYBRID** to local gyms and fellow personal
trainers. Audience is the **coach** (B2B2C), not end-consumers.

## What's in here

| File | What it is | Use it to… |
|------|------------|-----------|
| `coach-pitch-onepager.md` | One-page leave-behind | Email or hand to a coach/gym after they show interest; export to PDF for a clean handout. |
| `coach-outreach-scripts.md` | Cold / warm / follow-up messages | Start conversations by DM, email, or SMS. Personalize the `[brackets]`. |
| `coach-pitch-deck.md` | 10-slide deck outline + speaker notes | Run a 15-minute demo call or in-person pitch. |

## How to use it

1. **Reach out** with a script from `coach-outreach-scripts.md`. One ask: a 15-minute demo.
2. **Pitch** with `coach-pitch-deck.md`, ending on a live roster setup.
3. **Leave behind** the one-pager (`coach-pitch-onepager.md`) so they have it after.

**Export the one-pager to PDF:** open it in any Markdown editor (Typora, VS Code + Markdown PDF,
or `pandoc coach-pitch-onepager.md -o hybrid-onepager.pdf`). Drop in your brand colors when you
do — acid-lime `#c4f035` on near-black `#0c0d0c`.

## Stay honest (these were written into the copy on purpose)

- **The ask is free founding-coach early access.** Paid coaching seats are designed but not yet
  live — don't quote prices as if they're for sale. "Founder pricing locked in later" is the line.
- **Plan library:** Bodybuilding plans are fully built today; the other 18 goals are rolling out.
  Lean on the **program builder** (build your own, assign to many) — that's shipped and strong.
- **Squad monitor & team-compare** are on the web app today; full mobile versions are in progress.
  Fine to demo on web; don't promise them on the phone yet.

Keep these accurate as the product ships — update the copy when billing goes live and as the
plan library fills in.
