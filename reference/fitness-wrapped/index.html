01 · Cosmic Gains

PaletteVoid black & midnight indigo, electric cyan, violet, magenta, solar gold.
Hero metaphorThe universe. Effort as a solar system; muscles as a constellation.
StatsMinutes = huge gradient counter over a twinkling starfield. Exercises = a count at the core of a spinning solar system, planets orbiting it. Volume (kg) = mega number + "relatable mass" chips (buses, pianos). Muscles = constellation of glowing nodes sized by share, joined by dotted star-lines.
MotionSlow zoom-out reveals, parallax starfield drift, planets in continuous orbit, count-ups, dotted constellation lines drawing in.
Sample1,840 min · 247 exercises · 48,920 kg · Top: Chest, Back, Quads
MoodAwe, vastness, premium, "you are limitless."

02 · Neon Pulse

PaletteNear-black base, electric cyan, hot magenta/pink, acid lime, violet.
Hero metaphorAn overclocked machine / arcade HUD. You as a system running at max.
StatsMinutes = "uptime" glitch counter. Exercises = a live audio-equalizer bar field pulsing. Volume = "load output" number + skewed HUD cards. Muscles = skewed neon data bars ("power routing / target zones").
MotionGlitch/RGB-split on titles, scanlines, perspective grid floor scrolling, equalizer pulse, hard 0.35s cuts, slide-in from left.
Sample2,210 min · 312 exercises · 61,450 kg · Top: Shoulders, Arms, Core
MoodHigh-octane, rebellious, "viral on social," adrenaline.

03 · Forge of Fire

PaletteCoal & ash black, ember orange, molten gold, blood red, cool steel accent.
Hero metaphorA blacksmith's forge. You are iron being tempered; reps are hammer strikes.
StatsMinutes = glowing thin-weight number "under fire." Exercises = number + a grid of igniting spark marks (hammer strikes). Volume = a glowing molten ingot with a moving shine, value cast into it. Muscles = molten "temperature gauges" with lava fills and °heat readouts.
MotionRising embers, heat-shimmer breathing glow, ingot shine sweep, sparks popping in sequence, lava bars filling.
Sample1,560 min · 198 exercises · 73,200 kg · Top: Legs, Back, Glutes
MoodIntense, gritty, earned, "forged through fire," prestige.

04 · Wild Bloom

PaletteCream & soft greens, coral, sunshine yellow, sky blue, berry pink.
Hero metaphorA garden growing through a season. Sessions are seeds; progress blooms.
StatsMinutes = "time in the sun" green counter. Exercises = a tree that literally grows a canopy on slide entry. Distance (km) = counter + flower-petal stat cards (relatable journeys). Muscles = blooming flowers sized by share, with stems + sun centers.
MotionDrifting falling leaves, breathing sun halo, trunk + canopy growth, petals popping with spring easing, gentle scale-in.
Sample1,320 min · 176 exercises · 412 km · Top: Quads, Calves, Core
MoodJoyful, optimistic, wholesome, "you grew," shareable & warm.

05 · Concrete

PaletteOff-white paper, near-black ink, one acid-yellow + one red accent. No gradients.
Hero metaphorA brutalist data sheet / risograph poster. The raw, honest record.
StatsMinutes & Volume = colossal edge-to-edge numerals with hard rules. Exercises = a stark 10×10 tally grid filling in. Muscles = heavy outlined bar chart with mix-blend value labels. Final = a bordered summary table, one acid row.
MotionNo easing — hard cuts and instant fills, sequential reveals, tally cells stamping in. Deliberately anti-slick.
Sample1,975 min · 263 exercises · 55,310 kg · Top: Chest, Shoulders, Back
MoodConfident, no-nonsense, editorial, "the numbers speak," design-forward.
