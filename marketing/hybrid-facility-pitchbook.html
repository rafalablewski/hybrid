Situation

The economics of a gym are decided by two numbers.

Both are within your control — and both are currently managed by tools that weren't built for the job.

1 Retention is the business

Member lifetime value is dues × months retained. A typical facility loses ~3–5% of members every month — roughly a third of the base a year — and re-acquiring each one costs real marketing spend.²

Small changes compound: shaving one point off monthly churn can add months of lifetime to every member on the floor.

2 Coaching is the margin

Personal training and small-group are the highest-margin lines in the building — yet most coaches are capacity-capped by admin: spreadsheets, manual programming, no visibility across clients.

Every hour a coach spends building plans is an hour they can't sell or coach.

The platform — why it's defensible

A decision engine, not a content library.

  • HPI — Hybrid Performance Index. One 0–100 score fusing strength, endurance and recovery, with the limiter named — a shared language for coach and member.
  • Adaptive prescription. Loads adjust to each athlete's readiness (own HRV / RHR / sleep baselines) and accumulated fatigue. One plan, individualised for every body.
  • Velocity-based training. Capture bar speed, estimate 1RM without max testing, auto-prescribe today's working load.
  • Confidence compounds with data. The more a member logs, the sharper the engine — a moat that deepens the longer they stay on your floor.
