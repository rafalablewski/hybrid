WHY THIS CONCEPT

  • Stories rail first: puts people at the very top — the strongest pull-to-open hook from Instagram. Social isn't buried at the bottom; it greets you.
  • Rings as the universal object: Today = daily activity rings (Move/Train/Fuel); Cockpit = HPI sub-scores as concentric rings. One mental model spans both pages.
  • Trade-off: highest emotional/glance value, but rings compress detail — power users get less raw data above the fold. Best if engagement > analysis is the goal.
