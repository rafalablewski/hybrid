WHY THIS CONCEPT

  • Size = priority: the hero spans 2 columns; secondary metrics are 1×1 tiles. Hierarchy survives even when the user reorders or hides tiles — great for a future "customizable dashboard."
  • One vocabulary, two zoom levels: Today = action-leaning tiles, Cockpit = metric-leaning tiles, but identical tile grammar — minimal components to build, perfect web↔mobile parity.
  • Trade-off: maximum density and scannability, but weaker narrative flow — it's a wall of widgets, not a story. Social/feed content fits awkwardly (long text wants full-width rows, not square tiles).
