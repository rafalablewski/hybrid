Also today — the reduction pass

What can we remove? — 5 answers

The shipped ledger-footer card says its number three ways: a lime pill counting rows, a ledger line counting the day, a kicker that sometimes repeats the ledger's words. These five each take a different single-number, single-action position. Common cuts across all five: the lime pill is gone (a short list is its own count), the per-row ✓ is gone (everything in this card is by definition done), and the count is a real, honest tap target that opens the day's log. Every concept keeps the brief: always visible, count lives here, the current dashed bar where it earns its place.

1One line

The header absorbs the count

Kicker left, a quiet "1 today →" right — count and tap-through fused into one element where the pill used to sit. Rows are clean; the dashed bar is the card's only action, present in both states, so the card behaves identically full or empty.
