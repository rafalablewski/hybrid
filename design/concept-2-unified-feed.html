WHY THIS CONCEPT

  • One ranking engine, two lenses: the same priority model powers both pages — Today filters to "act," Cockpit to "analyze." No duplicated logic, no two layouts to maintain.
  • Priority chips do the hierarchy: DO-NOW > alerts > recap > social is encoded per-card, so the order can adapt daily (a flagged injury jumps the queue) without redesigning sections.
  • Trade-off: most flexible and scalable, but a uniform timeline can feel less "designed" / less distinct between pages — risks the two screens looking too similar. Needs strong chip + filter cues to stay legible.
