# HYBRID — coach outreach scripts

Ready-to-send messages for reaching gym owners and personal trainers. Replace everything in
`[brackets]`. Keep it short, lead with their problem, make one clear ask. Send the one-pager
only *after* they reply with interest — don't dump it in the first message.

---

## 1. Cold — gym owner (email or DM)

**Subject:** A smarter system for your coaching team

> Hi [name],
>
> I run [your gym/your name] and I've been using a coaching platform called **HYBRID** that's
> changed how I program for clients. You build a plan once, assign it to your whole roster, and
> it auto-adjusts each person's loads to their own readiness and fatigue — plus one screen shows
> you who's overreaching or dropping off before it becomes a problem.
>
> We're in free founding-coach early access right now. Worth a 15-minute look for [gym name]'s
> floor team?
>
> [your name]

---

## 2. Cold — fellow personal trainer (DM or email)

> Hey [name] — quick one. I've been running my clients on **HYBRID**, a coaching app built
> around an actual training-science engine (not just a workout logger). I build one program and
> it individualizes the loads for every client off their readiness and fatigue, and I get a
> single dashboard of who needs attention each day.
>
> It's free in early access while they onboard founding coaches. Want me to show you my roster
> on a quick call? I think it'd save you serious programming time.

---

## 3. Warm / referral (you share a connection)

> Hi [name] — [referrer] mentioned you're coaching [clients/athletes] and might like this. I've
> been using **HYBRID** to program for my roster; the part that sold me is that one plan adapts
> to each athlete automatically, and I can see everyone's readiness and injury-risk at a glance.
>
> They're letting founding coaches in free right now. Happy to walk you through how I've set
> mine up — 15 minutes this week?

---

## 4. Follow-up nudge (3–4 days, no reply)

> Hey [name], floating this back up. No pressure — if it's not the right time, all good. If you
> *are* curious, I can show you my actual roster in HYBRID in 15 minutes and you can decide from
> there. Want me to send a couple of times?

---

## 5. Ultra-short (SMS / Instagram DM)

> Hey [name] — using a coaching app called HYBRID that builds one program and auto-adjusts it
> per client off their readiness/fatigue, with a who-needs-attention dashboard. Free in early
> access. Worth a 15-min look? 👇

---

### Tips
- **One ask per message** — the demo. Don't pitch features in the first touch.
- **Lead with their pain** (admin time, generic plans, no roster visibility), not your tech.
- Say **"free founding-coach early access"** plainly — don't imply paid plans exist yet.
- Personalize the first line; generic openers get ignored.
- Follow up **once**, then leave it.
