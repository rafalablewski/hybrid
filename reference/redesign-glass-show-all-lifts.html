IDEA A

Expand in place

A classic disclosure — the card grows and the lifts appear inline. Familiar, zero context switch, but the card can get tall. Best when the list is short (5 is fine).

IDEA B

Peek — sense the rest

Never fully expand; instead signal that more exists — depth, dots, frost, or a shape. The card stays a fixed, calm height and the affordance is ambient. Great for a glanceable “today” glass card.

Open a surface
