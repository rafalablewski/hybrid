Three concepts, five layouts each. The argument is the left edge.

The oddness isn't the type or the spacing — it's that the card runs three different left edges at once, and every line picks one at random. Fix the edge and most of the "weird margins" disappear on their own. Each layout below holds a single edge and states which one.

Reading the set

02-B and 05-B are the two that fix the cause rather than the symptom. B keeps the indent and makes it honest — the ✓ hangs in its own margin and every line below, seam included, lands on 51px. 05-B deletes the indent and puts the whole block on a 26px baseline, which is why its vertical spacing reads as deliberate rather than assembled.

In the 07 set, A and E are the ones worth building: the plate has to be full-bleed. The moment it is inset (B) it introduces a fourth edge, which is the original problem with extra shadow.

One caution on 05-D and 02-C: both put content on a right edge, which is the one place the day stamp already lives. On a narrow phone with a six-figure day they will collide before anything else here does.
