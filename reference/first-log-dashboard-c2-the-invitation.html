The Invitation — empty days are the pitch

Marketing never says a word. After the first log the week rail appears, and its future days render as dashed, open slots. Tapping tomorrow doesn't show an error or an empty screen — it shows an honest state: "Monday is open." One primary action fills the week with a plan; one ghost action says "I'll just show up." The three onboarding cards disappear entirely after the first log — the calendar itself carries all three choices, placed exactly where the user's eye already is: on the day they're thinking about.

The design argument

The most persuasive advertisement for a plan is the absence it fills. A dashed circle around Monday says more than a card ever could: here is a day, it belongs to you, it is currently silent. We let the void do the selling — quietly, in sand, never in guilt.
