The ambient interface wearing the app's actual chrome — the three-column AppHeader, the Today hub's glyph pills, the split bottom nav with its detached verb circle, RADIUS's five rungs and the 72×72 monoline glyphs, all read off core/app-header.ts, today-tabs.ts, nav-bar.ts, geometry.ts and theme/icons.ts. Try this, in order

Each step changes numbers on the other screens. The point is not the screen you are on — it is what moves everywhere else.

Change the morning

The prescription is computed from sleep and resting heart rate, not hard-coded. Flip the night and the coach changes its mind — same engine, opposite instruction.

What is your kit, unchanged

The AppHeader's 44dp tiles with the wordmark centred by construction and the streak on its second line. The hub pills carrying glyphs rather than three words of unequal length, with a neutral selection — not the brand chartreuse. The masthead's fixed 15dp meta row holding the title's y steady across all three tabs. The split nav: four places in the capsule, the verb detached beside it in the accent, morphing per surface exactly as auroraNavAction specifies — Train on Today, Add post on Feed, Say on Nutrition. Radii come from the five rungs and nowhere else.

What is actually simulated

Readiness falls out of sleep debt, resting-HR delta and accumulated muscle cost. The prescription is that readiness mapped to a load factor and rounded to the plate. Freshness decays per working set and takes a 9-point hit from the run. The protein target scales with tonnage actually moved, so finishing your session raises it while you stand there. HPI is the three pillars weighted, which is why a hard session pushes strength up and recovery down and the headline barely moves.

Real HYBRID does this properly in readiness.ts, effort.ts, recovery-pairs.ts, landmark-adapt.ts and hpi.ts. These are toy versions with the same shape.

What is faked, honestly

Voice is a menu, not a microphone — press the accent circle and pick an utterance. The run is fourteen seconds rather than forty-six minutes. The film is real motion graphics but the narration is text, not audio. Nothing here needs a camera, which is the finding rather than a limitation.
