01 · Executive summary
The thesis

A coach-led wedge into the fitness market.

Cold consumer installs convert at 1–2% and churn fast. A platform a coach hands you in person — and uses with you every week — converts an order of magnitude better and retains because the relationship, not the app, is the lock-in. So the entry strategy is B2B2C: land the coach (recurring seat revenue), embed in the gym (free distribution + retention story), and let both pull the members onto the consumer tiers. Each (5 gyms + 5 coaches) set is a self-contained, profitable “beachhead unit” that one founder/rep can close in 8 weeks and then replicate.

02 · Value creation
Who benefits, and how

Three beneficiaries. One aligned system.

The model only scales if every party is structurally better off. The member's experience (adaptive sessions, PRs, recaps, accountability) is the engine of retention — but the three parties who decide whether HYBRID wins are the gym, the coach, and us.

For the gym

Retention & differentiation — at zero cost

  • Churn radar: the Accountability Engine flags members going quiet before they cancel — directly defends membership revenue.
  • “Coached gym” positioning: every membership ships with an adaptive app rivals can't match — a sales differentiator.
  • Higher PT attach: the app makes coaching visibly valuable, lifting personal-training sales (the gym's highest-margin line).
  • Zero ops, optional upside: no hardware, no integration; optional revenue share on coach seats sold in their gym.
For the coach

More clients, less admin, recurring online income

  • Scale past floor hours: hold up to 40 athletes on one seat, including remote & travelling clients.
  • Build once, assign to many: author a program, push it to a client or a whole group in two taps.
  • The weekly check-in loop: the ritual that converts one-off plans into monthly recurring client revenue.
  • Adaptive does the grunt work: loads auto-adjust to each client's recovery between sessions — the coach supervises, doesn't recompute.
For us (the company)

Recurring revenue, near-zero CAC, a data moat

  • Predictable B2B MRR: coach seats are sticky, expand with roster size, and carry ~96% margin.
  • Consumer upsell, organically: members self-convert Casual → Athlete once hooked — no paid acquisition.
  • Capital-efficient GTM: 1,650 warm users from 10 in-person conversations; the unit is replicable.
  • Compounding data: every logged session & check-in refits the engine — better adaptation → higher retention → cheaper growth.

04 · Revenue integrity
The leakage question

“What stops people fake-joining a coach to get Pro free?”

It's the right question — coached clients get Full included, so the obvious exploit is a group of friends nominating a “coach” and splitting one seat instead of each paying $12.99. The architecture closes this on three fronts: access is gated, capacity is paid, and the entitlement is conditional.

There is no free Full. It is always funded — either by the athlete ($12.99/$99) or by a coach's capped, pre-paid seat tied to a verified, consented, active relationship.
1

Coach is a verified role, not a toggle Live

You can't self-declare as a coach. A user applies, an admin reviews credentials and approves — the role promotion is audited. No verified coach → no seat → no entitlement to hand out. This alone kills the “be your own coach” exploit.

2

Mutual-consent, audited linking Live

A client is attached only via a two-sided CoachLink — the coach initiates, the client accepts (or vice-versa), and it's logged. Nobody is silently added; every relationship has a paper trail an operator can inspect.

3

Capacity is finite and pre-paid Live

Every seat holds a hard cap (10 / 40 / 150). Each client granted Full consumes a slot the coach already paid for. There is no marginal “free” entitlement — the coach buys the headroom up front. Even at the worst-case arbitrage ($79 ÷ 40 ≈ $1.98/client), the company still collects the seat fee, locks the users in, and captures their data.

4

Entitlement is bound to an ACTIVE link Live

Full is granted by resolving the persona from a live relationship. End or expire the CoachLink and the client reverts to Casual (free) immediately. There is no persistent, detached Pro to farm.

5

Engagement gate — the clincher Recommended

Seat-funded Full persists only while a real coaching touch exists: at least one assigned plan/program or check-in reply within a rolling 30-day window. A dormant “client” with zero coaching activity drops to Casual. This forces any would-be arbitrage coach to actually coach 40 people — at which point it isn't fake, it's the business working as intended.

6

Abuse analytics & operator review Recommended

Flag seats where many clients share a device/IP, never receive a plan, or sit on an uncredentialed coach. The audit trail already exists; this layers detection on top so outliers surface for manual review.

Net effect: the exploit costs the exploiter a verified application, a paid seat slot, and ongoing coaching activity — to save ~$11/mo per person. The juice isn't worth the squeeze, and the few who try still pay us and feed the network. Leakage is bounded by design, not policed by hope.

The beachhead is a unit, not a one-off.

The whole point of standardising the (5 gyms + 5 coaches) motion is that it repeats. One person can run a unit in ~8 weeks; the same script, offer and case studies make every subsequent unit faster to close.

07 · The product
What you actually demo

Not a tracker. An adaptive coaching system.

The five things to show in the gym office — all shipped, web and iPhone.

01

Adaptive prescription

Working loads dosed from the athlete's own lifts and today's recovery (readiness + HPI). The plan moves with the person — the part a spreadsheet can't do.

02

Coach console

Build a plan or multi-week program, push it to a client or a whole group at once. Mutual-consent, private coach↔client.

03

Weekly check-in

The retention heartbeat: client submits metrics + note, coach reviews and replies in-app. The reason clients pay monthly.

04

Come-back loop

PR celebrations, shareable recaps, streaks, the Future-Self simulator, and an Accountability Engine that predicts the quit and nudges.

05

Free plan library

Curated goal-first plans a member can follow free — value before anyone pays, and the top of the consumer funnel.

08 · Execution
The 8-week ground game

Land coaches first, then gyms, then the floor.

Weeks 1–2Coaches

Sign 5 coaches on founding seats

Demo the console live, set up their first program, onboard 2–3 clients in the room. Free 90-day founding seat in exchange for a testimonial + one case study. Start with the people who pay you.

Weeks 2–4Gyms

Walk into 5 gym offices with proof

You're no longer selling an idea — a coach in their own gym is already using it. Pitch retention + “coached gym” positioning, free, with optional seat revenue share. Agree a member launch date.

Weeks 4–6Members

Activate the floor

Staffed table for a week: scan → install → follow a free plan → log session one, same visit. Bring the coaches; members trust them. Capture testimonials while energy is high.

Weeks 6–8Systematize

Measure, package, repeat

Pull installs, 30-day actives, check-in streaks, PRs. Turn the best into a one-page case study — the asset that makes unit #2 a 20-minute close. Turn billing on as trials end.
