HYBRID — Logging redesign 01

Liquid Glass — refined

Your guidance applied. The active set is still a single bright glass slab floating over the aurora, but the interaction is cleaner:

  • Log = one plain + button. No labelled pill — you thumb the big lime circle to bank the set. It sits right next to the numbers.
  • Add set & Special, redesigned. The dashed box and the ▾ menu are gone — twin glass tiles: a ringed + Add set and a ⚡ Special for warm-ups, drop sets, AMRAP, failure.
  • Plan sets in advance (right phone). Queued sets show inline under the active one as ghosted rows; the planner sheet lets you set a count, pick a shape (straight / pyramid / warm-up ramp) and lay out the whole exercise before you lift.
