# HYBRID — coach pitch deck (outline)

A 10-slide deck for an in-person or Zoom pitch to a gym owner or fellow PT. Each slide has the
on-screen line(s) plus speaker notes. Keep slides sparse — you talk, the slide anchors. Aim for
12–15 minutes, leaving room for the live roster demo at the end.

Brand: acid-lime `#c4f035` accents on near-black `#0c0d0c`, Archivo type, mono for numbers.

---

## Slide 1 — Title

**On screen:** HYBRID — Strength & conditioning for hybrid athletes
*The operating system for the coaches who train them.*

**Notes:** One sentence: "I'm going to show you how to program for your whole roster in a
fraction of the time, and never lose track of who needs you." Set the 15-minute expectation.

---

## Slide 2 — The problem

**On screen:** You're running a real business on spreadsheets and generic apps.

**Notes:** Name their day: rewriting plans per client, apps that give everyone the same program,
no single view of who's overreaching or fading. The pain scales with the roster — the better
you do, the worse the admin gets. Get them nodding before you show anything.

---

## Slide 3 — The shift

**On screen:** Most apps sell *content*. HYBRID is a *decision system*.

**Notes:** A workout app hands out exercises. HYBRID reads each athlete's training, readiness,
and fatigue and tells you what to do next — and why. You stay the coach; the engine does the
load math. This is the core reframe — land it before features.

---

## Slide 4 — Build once, assign to many

**On screen:** One program → your whole roster. Each client gets it individualized.

**Notes:** Program builder: weeks → days → exercises (sets × reps × RPE). Push to one client or
a whole group; it lands on each calendar as dated sessions. Then the key line: the engine
adjusts each athlete's working loads to *their* readiness and fatigue — one plan, twenty bodies,
zero rewrites.

---

## Slide 5 — The squad monitor

**On screen:** One screen. Who needs you today.

**Notes:** Roster ranked worst-first by readiness, training-load ratio (ACWR), and injury flags,
auto-segmented into needs-attention / dormant / new / on-track. You walk onto the floor already
knowing where your attention goes. This is the "aha" for anyone managing more than a few clients.

---

## Slide 6 — The engine (why it's different)

**On screen:** HPI · readiness · velocity-based loads · confidence that grows with data.

**Notes:** Plain-language tour:
- **HPI** — one 0–100 score across strength, endurance, recovery, with the limiter named.
- **Readiness** — built from each athlete's *own* HRV/RHR/sleep baselines, not population averages.
- **VBT** — bar speed → estimated 1RM without max testing → today's prescribed load.
- The kicker: the more they log, the more confident the prescriptions get. "Bloomberg terminal
  for the body." Don't over-explain the math — sell that it's real and tested.

---

## Slide 7 — Keep the relationship close

**On screen:** Private notes · shared check-ins · benchmark the whole team on any lift.

**Notes:** Private coaching notes the client never sees. Weekly check-ins they share, you reply.
Team-compare lines everyone up on any lift to spot who's progressing or stalling. The tech makes
you *more* present, not less.

---

## Slide 8 — Web + mobile

**On screen:** Coach from the desk or the gym floor. Athletes train on either.

**Notes:** Full web app and native iOS/Android. You manage rosters anywhere; clients train
anywhere. One product, two screens, same data.

---

## Slide 9 — Founding-coach early access

**On screen:** Free now. Shape the roadmap. Founder pricing locked in later.

**Notes:** Be honest and confident: we're onboarding a first group of coaches **free**. Bring
your real roster today. Your feedback sets what we build next (plan library, integrations).
When paid seats launch, founding coaches keep a permanent preferred rate. No cost, no catch —
we want serious coaches stress-testing it.

---

## Slide 10 — The ask

**On screen:** Let's set up your roster — right now.

**Notes:** Close with action, not "let me know." Offer to create their account and add 2–3 real
clients live on the call so they leave already using it. Capture: best email, roster size, what
they coach. Then run the live demo.
