What changed

The old Home tab showed a Performance State card, a week rail, an exercise widget and a plan strip, and asked the athlete to synthesise them. This screen commits to a position — one instruction, one adjusted number, one action — and puts the synthesis behind a plus.

The struck-through 100 matters more than the 92.5. It says the plan said one thing and I overruled it, which is the single behaviour that separates a coach from a calendar.

Every driver carries its provenance

The four rows under the fold reuse the four-layer vocabulary learned.ts already speaks — population, profile, observed, measured. An athlete who wants to argue with the number can see exactly which layer it came from, and "measured" outranking "observed" is just the device-truth rule surfaced.

The escalation ladder, on one screen

Each rung is used only when the one above fails. Most sets end at rung one or two — that is the entire product idea, and the reason this can ship without a camera or a microphone breakthrough.

1 Silence. The prescription was right, the IMU saw the bar rack and the movement stop, the rest timer started itself. Logged.
2 A gesture. "Same as last week?" — nod. AirPods head gesture, Watch double-tap.
3 A word. "eight" — "two left" — "add five".
4 A sentence. "my shoulder's cranky, give me something else".
5 The screen. Always available, never required.

Why the numbers are this big

Because the phone is on a bench four feet away and the athlete is upside down under a bar. 72px is not a style choice, it is a legibility requirement — and it is the one place this design deliberately leaves the type scale, the way scale.ts already permits for art rather than reading.

The honest limit

Voice is never the only path. A large minority of people will not talk to their phone in a public gym, and gyms are loud. Every utterance here has a one-tap equivalent on the screen behind it, permanently.

set boundary phone IMU — motion stop, no CV
rest timer starts itself, never asked for
speech on-device, closed grammar, LLM only on a miss
during the set the coach says nothing. Not once.

The one interaction a general model cannot copy

Ask any frontier model whether to do another set and it will give a competent, generic answer. It cannot say 0.85×, because that number is four months of this athlete's own recovery measured against a population curve — and it cannot know that the ceiling estimate has converged over 23 observations.

This is the entire product thesis in one sheet. Not "we have an AI coach". "We have four months of you."

It says when it does not know

If the landmark replay reports the ceiling has not settled, the fourth row reads not yet, n=6 and the verdict softens to a suggestion. An engine that admits its own uncertainty earns more trust than one that is confidently wrong once.

This is the "video out", and it is not generative

Every frame above is deterministic motion graphics driven by real engine output, with a synthesised voice over the top. It renders server-side for cents, it cannot hallucinate, and recap.ts and learned.ts already compute every number in it.

Compare that with generating photoreal video of the athlete: expensive, slow, uncanny, and one clip showing a rep they never did ends the brand. There is no version of that worth the risk.

Why this is a new content category

It is the first fitness media that is about you and true. Not a filtered highlight reel — a film of your own physiology, narrated in the second person, that you could not have made yourself and cannot get anywhere else.

It is also the most shareable object the company can produce, which makes it the retention loop and the acquisition loop at the same time.

The only camera bet worth taking

Not form coaching — attestation. packages/core/src/attestation.ts already declares this six-tier ladder and tiers 0 to 2 have shipped. Tier 3 is a narrow, bounded camera problem: one declared attempt, framing the athlete wants to get right, and a single question — how many plates, and did the rep complete.

The failure mode is benign. "Couldn't verify, try again" costs nothing. "Your knee is caving" costs trust and invites liability.

Why this is the moat and the film is not

Every strength number on the internet is self-reported and therefore worth nothing. There is no strength equivalent of a verified marathon time. A credential is worth what its recognition is worth — which makes it a graph, and graphs are the only thing on the moat list with real network effects.

The film is the most magical screen here. This is the most valuable one. Build it fourth.

shipped tiers 0 claimed — 1 sensed — 2 witnessed
the bet tier 3 recorded — plate count, not form score
exit ringed arrow — it leaves. Never a bordered box.

Three tests, all three must pass

Decision-changing — it alters what the athlete does in the next 24 hours. Non-obvious — they would not have worked it out themselves. Defensible — the evidence fits on one line.

The message above passes all three and is the single highest-value sentence the product can say. The four below it fail, and three of them are what every competitor sends.

Structural guards, not a settings screen

Hard cap: one proactive message a day. If the athlete ignores three of a type, that type stops permanently — no re-prompting, no "are you sure". And it arrives when they wake, not when it was computed at 3 a.m.

If the day is unremarkable, say nothing. A coach who is quiet when there is nothing to say is a coach you believe when they speak.

  • Now — the claim, the session line, the receipt.
  • Record — verified lifts, the films, the archive. The trophy cabinet.
  • Voice — everything else. Authoring screens are reached by asking.
  • Plan editing, exercise browsing and food logging keep full traditional UI, unapologetically. Authoring is a screen job.

The honest caveat. This is the most speculative screen here. Navigation is not only wayfinding — it is browsing, discovery and accountability, and a product that hides its own data reads as unaccountable the first time it tells you something you disagree with. The AI decides the default; the screen exists so the athlete can inspect and override it. Ship the Today card first and watch whether anyone misses the tabs before deleting them.
