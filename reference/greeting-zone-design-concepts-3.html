HYBRID — Today screen — round three

8 concepts × both scenarios

Same eight ideas, now stress-tested against reality: each concept renders Scenario A (no plan — the frozen chooser cards) and Scenario B (enrolled — the frozen plan card with its week rail and Start). The greeting treatment has to be one system that survives both states, because the athlete crosses from A to B the day they enrol — and back, the day a plan ends. Both card blocks are untouched in every mock; only the strip above them changes.

01 — FLIP THE HIERARCHY

The Inversion

The greeting becomes a warm sand kicker; the headline slot belongs to whatever the screen is actually about. No plan: the question “How do you want to start?” takes the slot. On a plan: the slot goes empty and the plan card’s own title becomes the de-facto headline — the kicker plus one meta line is the entire zone.

Plan-state behaviour — this is the concept’s best argument: after enrolling, nothing above the card competes with “6-Day Push/Pull/Legs”. The greeting never fights your plan for attention.

02 — THE APP STORE MOVE

Today

Caption date, one enormous Today, the greeting as a small warm sentence. The masthead is byte-for-byte identical in both scenarios — only what sits beneath it changes, which is exactly the point: the page identity is stable while the content swaps.

Plan-state behaviour — “FREE” simply vanishes from the caption line (an enrolled athlete doesn’t need it), and “Today” reads even better over a concrete session than over a chooser.

Addendum — scrubbing the week rail: the rule 02 needs

A static “Today” over Friday’s session would lie in the largest type on the screen. So the masthead lives: today and its neighbours get relative names (“Yesterday”, “Today”, “Tomorrow”); anything further gets the weekday name — one strong word that survives EN/PL/DE, with the relative distance as a small tag on the caption line. The greeting sentence exists only on today; on any other day its slot becomes the way back.

The full rule: selected day = today → “Today” + caption date + greeting. ±1 day → “Yesterday” / “Tomorrow”. Beyond ±1 → the weekday name (“Friday”, “Tuesday”), never “2 days ago” — clumsy as a headline and worse inflected in Polish and German; the distance rides the caption line as a mono tag instead. The greeting appears only on today; elsewhere its line becomes “Back to today →”, which doubles as the return affordance — so the headline itself is the loudest possible signal that you’ve left today, and the way home is always one tap, in the same place. Past days show what was logged (or that nothing was); future days show the upcoming plan day with Start replaced by a preview state. The same rule ports to 03 (the big serif date tracks the viewed day) and 07 (the spine label becomes “Day 3 of 6”).

03 — A TRAINING JOURNAL COVER

Masthead

The big serif 19 with the month and greeting stacked behind a hairline — today’s journal cover. Identical in both scenarios; the no-plan state adds the question as a mono kicker matched to “FREE”, the plan state needs no kicker at all.

Plan-state behaviour — the serif date and the week rail’s lime “19” now rhyme: the masthead names the day, the rail locates it in the week. Two views of the same fact, no duplication of words.

04 — BRAUN ON THE FACEPLATE

Engraved

The greeting is the page’s one voice; zone labels are wide-tracked uppercase engravings that can’t compete. No plan: the engraving reads HOW DO YOU WANT TO START with “FREE” balancing it. On a plan: an engraving would only repeat what the card already says — so it disappears entirely.

Plan-state behaviour — the honest test of a label system: when the content names itself, the label is deleted, not re-worded. Greeting, date, card. Three elements, done.

05 — BORROW THE BRAND’S OWN MOTIF

The Rule

The lime dash from under HYBRID. becomes the zone divider. No plan: the dash anchors a fence line carrying the question and “FREE”. On a plan: the same dash-and-line runs wordless — pure structure marking “here ends the greeting, here begins today” — because the plan card supplies its own words.

Plan-state behaviour — one gesture, two voltages: with words when the zone needs naming, silent when it doesn’t. The athlete learns it once and reads it forever.

06 — MAXIMUM COMPRESSION

One Line

Greeting left, date right, one letterhead baseline — in both scenarios. No plan: “FREE” right-aligns quietly above the stack. On a plan: nothing else at all, and the entire plan card — including the Start button — rises roughly a hundred pixels toward the fold.

Plan-state behaviour — this is where the concept earns its keep: the enrolled athlete opens the app and Start is comfortably above the fold with the Done-today card peeking in. Space returned to training, daily.

07 — BIND, DON’T HEADLINE

The Spine

A thin chartreuse spine in the left margin binds a zone to its label. No plan: it runs from the small question down the chooser stack. On a plan: it runs from a small Day 1 of 6 waypoint down the plan card — the spine becomes today’s thread, and its length is literally how much today holds.

Plan-state behaviour — the label the spine carries changes from a question (“how do you want to start?”) to a position (“Day 1 of 6”), which is exactly what changed in the athlete’s life. Structure mirrors state.

08 — THE GREETING AS AN EVENT

Settle

On open the greeting appears alone, centered and generous; after ~600 ms it settles into a compact one-line row as the content rises. The choreography is identical in both scenarios — only what rises differs: the chooser for a new athlete, the plan card for an enrolled one. The settled layout is Concept 06.

Plan-state behaviour — arguably better than in the no-plan state: the daily ritual becomes hello → your session slides up → Start. Reduced-motion users get the settled frame directly.

Reading the two-scenario test: the concepts that get stronger on a plan are 01 (nothing competes with the plan title), 05 (the wordless rule), 06 (Start rises above the fold) and 07 (the spine's label becomes “Day 1 of 6”). Concepts 02 and 03 are the most stable — identical mastheads in both states. Concept 04's plan state collapses into plain greeting + card, which is honest but loses its signature. My pick across both scenarios is 05 (The Rule), with 01 (The Inversion) as the bold alternative — both ship to web and mobile together per the parity rule.
