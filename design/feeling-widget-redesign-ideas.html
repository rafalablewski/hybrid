Today home — "How ready do you feel?" card

The feeling card, re-thought — 5 ideas

Redesigns of the full-width home card (FeelingCard in today.tsx): the four readiness faces that log a check-in on tap, plus the DONE-TODAY session ring and the "Already trained?" link. Today it just lights one face and forgets — these carry the new model: log at least once a day, re-log anytime, capped once per 6h. Where the state matters, both the Unlocked and Cooling-down looks are shown.

1Logged-state aware

Smallest fix: it remembers today

Keeps the four faces exactly, but adds a status line — what you last logged, when — and a chip that flips between "Update anytime" and a live cooldown. The lit face shows today's pick instead of resetting to blank. Both states below.
