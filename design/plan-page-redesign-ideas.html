How to choose. Everything shares concept 1's frame, so these compose instead of compete: 1 is the shippable baseline; add 9 (Dock) for the missing enroll CTA, then pick ONE information slot — 2 (Wave), 4 (Countdown), 7 (Your Numbers) or 10 (Spec) — and optionally swap the week head for 8 (Phases). My pick: 1 + 9 + 7, with 8's phase bar. Build note: day cards need a "headline lift + token ramp" summary derived in planProgramView() (core) so web and mobile render identically, with the full AM/PM matrix behind a tap.
