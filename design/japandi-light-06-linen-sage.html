Linen & Sage

The brown moves out of the lead role. Sage — already the theme's secondary — becomes the single action colour, and the ground turns to pale linen with a green undertone, so the whole screen reads like morning light through a plant. The remaining warmth is one whisper of blush in accent text. Newsreader gives the headings an unhurried, editorial softness.
