HYBRID — Train — Plan detail

The individual plan page, 10 hero-led redesigns

The current page opens with a small title, a pipe-separated meta line, and a form — the maxes panel — before showing any of the plan. Every concept below gives Soviet 8-Week Peaking a big hero that answers "what is this plan and why should I run it?" in the first screenful, then hands off to the existing week selector and day cards. All mockups use the live brand tokens (ink, chalk, lime, mono) and real program data.

2The Wave

The program's own data is the hero

Phase bands underneath explain the block at a glance. The engine already computes NL per week, so this is honest, not decorative.

5The Cover

Album-art header that collapses

Music-app grammar: generative cover art per plan (here, concentric % rings in lime), title over a scrim, and a play-style enroll button. On scroll the art collapses into a compact sticky bar with the title and the button — the CTA never leaves the screen. Art is generated from plan data, so every program gets a unique cover for free.

How to choose. Ideas 2 (Wave), 7 (Your Numbers) and 8 (Phases) add real information the current page lacks — and they compose: a Wave or Phases hero with Your-Numbers chips beneath is one coherent page. 1 (Poster), 3 (Slab) and 9 (Glass) are visual-only upgrades shippable without touching the data layer. 4 (Countdown) needs the meet-date affordance; 5 (Cover) needs generated art per plan; 6 (Provenance) and 10 (Plate) are strong but plan-specific voices. Whatever wins, the hero pattern must render from planProgramView() in core so web and mobile ship it together, and the maxes form should collapse to chips in every variant.
