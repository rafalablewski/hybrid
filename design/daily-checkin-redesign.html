Recovery — Daily check-in

Daily check-in, rebuilt — 5 full-screen versions

Total redesign of the check-in screen. Same data captured — Energy, Sleep, Soreness, Mood, weight, an optional note, share-with-coach — presented five very different ways. All fix the framing bug in the current screen (it says "Daily" but reads "training volume this week" and "how did the week go?") and align with the new model: log at least once a day, re-log up to once per 6h.

Current bug: titled Daily but body says “this week” Fixed → today-framed copy throughout 1–5 number pills → expressive faces / sliders

One question at a time

Turns the wall of pills into a calm, swipeable flow — one big question per card with a face that reacts as you pick. Progress bar up top, "Skip" for anything you don't track. Lowest cognitive load; feels like the app is talking to you.
