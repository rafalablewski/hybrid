HYBRID — design study

A page for every sport.

Sixty-five sports live in the catalog and none of them has a home — they are chips in a picker on a shared screen. This gives each one its own page, built from a single template that configures itself from the sport's own data: what it measures decides what the page shows. Running gets kilometres and a per-kilometre pace. Swimming gets metres and a per-hundred pace. A sport that only records time gets neither, and is not given a fabricated one.

Shipped. Both clients render this from one self-configuring model in @hybrid/core — sportPageModel(). The Sport screen is now the index that lifts into it, and Today's sport tiles open a sport's page directly.

Scroll inside a phone — the hero collapses on the shipped track.

What the page is built on

Three decisions

The unit is the identity

Every sport page wears the same chartreuse, because in this app colour encodes state and never section. What separates Running from Swimming is the measurement — km against metres, /km against /100 m, a 5 km time against a 100 m time. The sport announces itself in its own numbers.

One number, then the evidence

The page opens on the marker the sport is actually judged by, at the top of the type ladder, alone. Totals, volume, pace and the strength work all sit under it as support. Nothing else on the page is allowed to be that large.

Sections earn their place

Pace exists only for a sport that records pace. The strength block exists only for the seven sports that carry a transfer pool. A section is never rendered empty and never filled with an invented metric — the same rule the endurance lanes already hold.

Anatomy

Top to bottom
01

Cover hero

Rank cover from the Hero System — a sport is one nameable thing, so it gets art, a wash and an emblem, not a large title. 252 pt at rest, collapsing on the one track to the 56 pt bar; the back button never moves. Fixed-dark in both themes: a cover is a printed object.

02

Eyebrow, title, meta

The three hero slots and nothing else. Eyebrow names the kind of thing (the catalog category); the meta line carries facts about this instance, joined with a spaced en dash. The level sits in the rail's accessory slot as a control, not as a fourth fact.

03

The marker

The sport's own performance marker from the catalog — a 5 km time, a 100 m time, an FTP — with its delta and its provenance. Where a watch recorded it, the line says so in white: the device's read is the truth, and the mark is never tinted.

04

Totals

Four facts on hairlines rather than four cards. The distance cell exists only when the sport records distance, and it reads in the sport's own unit — metres for the pool, kilometres for the road.

05

Volume and pace

Eight weeks, the same buckets the endurance hub already computes, so a bar and a trend point can never disagree. The pace axis is reversed — lower is faster — and the personal best carries a dot.

06

Effort

Easy, steady and hard as one bar in three densities of the single accent. Three hues would imply three meanings; this is one meaning at three intensities, derived from pace against the athlete's own best.

07

Bests

A full-bleed rail — the cards run under the physical screen edge and align with the content column at rest, matching the exercise-widget standard.

08

Transfer

The demands ranked as the data ranks them, then today's three blocks dosed off the athlete's own logged e1RM. Change the level and the prescription re-doses live — it is the shipped engine, not a picture of one.

09

Recent efforts

The last three sessions, each reading through the device projection where one exists, so the pace on this row and the pace in the workout are the same second-accurate number.

When the sport measures less

Tennis — timed only

Fifty of the catalog's sixty-five sports record nothing but duration, and seven carry a strength pool. The template answers by subtraction: no distance, so no volume in kilometres and no bests in distance; no pace, so no trend and no effort split; no transfer pool, so no strength block. The marker slot does not go empty — it falls back to the truest number the sport does have, which is time on court.

The same page on web

Parity — one product, two clients

The hero stops collapsing because a desktop page has no thumb to track, and the single column becomes two: the measured read on the left, the work to do on the right. Every token, every rung of the type ladder and every section rule is the same.

What shipped, and what it left open

Status
✓

A page per sport, both clients

One model in @hybrid/core decides what a sport shows; web and mobile render it and nothing else. The Sport screen became the index — your sports first, then the ones we prescribe for, the whole catalog behind a search — and Today's sport tiles open the page directly.

✓

Every sport has an address

Web ?s=sportpage&sport=<slug>, mobile /sport-page — so a sport page survives a refresh, a bookmark and a paste, and a link from one client opens on the other. Every surface that names a sport (Today's tiles, the Cockpit's Sport module) now lands on that sport, not on the picker.

✓

The marker keeps a history

A marker is typed, not derived, so the delta and the sparkline appear only from the SECOND typing. Up to 24 are kept, under the one store shape both clients write.

✓

A hold is dosed in seconds

The engine now doses each movement in the measure the exercise database says it is: reps stay reps, a hold becomes 3 × 20 s (growing with the level, since there are no reps to take away), a carry becomes 3 × 20 m. Neither gets a load — the engine derives load from an e1RM, and a hold has none it can know.

✓

One destination per depth

All your endurance (the hub) → one sport (this page) → one move (the exercise page). The hub's discipline picker and its duplicate charts are gone: it is the comparison now, every discipline side by side, each lane opening that sport's page.

✓

The mark is drawn

Fifteen stroke marks in the icon set's own 72-unit box, resolved per sport where its instrument is distinctive and per category otherwise. Both Hero Systems take drawn art now, so the mark holds its shape at 152 pt where a desaturated emoji dissolved.
