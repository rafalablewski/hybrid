HYBRID — design exploration

Six ways the readiness card could work.

Concepts only — none of these is built. The shipped card (r4) is the four faces, one state line, and a readings record. Each concept below keeps every behaviour the engine needs — a gate, a record that never overwrites, a governing read — and spends the card's space differently. All are drawn in the Aurora system on the same day: trained 08:00–09:00, logged Flat at 10:00, logged Good at 20:00.

If we change one thing: 03 The Drain is the only concept that earns its space by teaching something — it shows why a later answer counts more instead of asserting it, and it is not copyable without the model underneath. 05 The Ledger is the safe reduction if the card simply needs to be quieter.

Faces use the shipped SVG paths (apps/web/components/aurora/readiness-face.tsx). Tokens from packages/core/src/theme/tokens.ts. Convention follows design/*.html — render standalone concepts, pick one, record the choice in capabilities.ts.
