The Today header is inherited, untouched

Avatar tile, HYBRID. wordmark, the terra streak line, the bell with its badge, and the full-width three-segment control — exactly as shipped. The feed adds nothing above the fold: it is simply the third segment's state, and its own chrome begins below (For you / Following / Clubs).

Glass is chrome, never content

Header and nav use the shipped recipe — blur 14 / sat 1.8 / bright 1.08 over a 6% film. Cards stay flat and hairlined: material depth belongs to what floats, not what scrolls.

Co-sign opens the feed

A person is waiting on this answer, so it outranks all content. The claim is server-snapshotted — the witness signs exactly what they saw. Every request is also an invite.

The PR card earns its size

Only P0 moments get the 46 pt figure and the spark. The tier chip sits beside the number it proves, not by the name — provenance belongs to the claim, identity to the tick.

One accent per card

Discipline sets it: lime strength, teal conditioning, slate coach, sand sport. Everything else stays chalk and ash — which is why the accent still means something.

Ordinary sessions stay quiet

Marek's Tuesday renders at title size with three top sets, not a ledger. The watch glyph marks device-measured figures; typed ones carry no mark and no shame.

The exit is the point

The caught-up marker ends the ranked stream honestly and hands you back to the bar: Go train deep-links today's session. Session starts are the KPI, not dwell.

Kyoto Hour — For you
washi grounds, pine accent, serif headlines — the shipped light re-voicing

A second voice, same skeleton

Kyoto Hour is not an inversion: washi #f6f3ea grounds, pine #44584c carries every job lime does in the dark, and headlines switch to the serif heading face per the shipped theme (--font-heading under [data-theme="light"]).

Glass gets denser in daylight

The 6% film reads as fog on light grounds, so light chrome runs a 45% white film with the same blur and rim — the shipped light-theme glass variables do exactly this.

Accents hold their meaning

Teal→indigo for conditioning, slate→indigo for coach, sand→bronze, terra→vermilion: the light accentText map keeps every accent ≥ 4.5:1 on washi. Meaning survives the theme; hue adapts to it.

Provenance climbs by removing the keyboard. Absence is the T0 mark — no scarlet letter. Weight rises with proof: figures render 600 → 700 → 800 as tiers climb.

Liquid Glass — the chrome material

"A nearly clear body whose identity lives at the rim." The shipped recipe, live below: 6% film, blur 14, saturate 1.8, brightness 1.08, inner rim highlight. Reduced transparency swaps it for a 94% solid.
