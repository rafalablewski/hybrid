Tennis / 75 min + Tennis 75 min 75 min as the hero figure, "Tennis" once in the meta line

A single-block sport log repeated its name and time by construction — the block was named after the sport. The block line is gone; the duration is promoted to the headline.

0.6 km – 31 min + 612 m, 31 min, 5:04 /100m 612 m hero, then "31 min – 5:04 /100m"

Distance was stated in two unit systems and time twice. Now distance appears once, in the sport's natural unit (metres for swimming/rowing, km otherwise), with time and pace each said once.
