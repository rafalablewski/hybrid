Today home — Feel widget

The Feel widget, re-thought — 5 fresh ideas

Scoped to the square widget on the Today home screen, the one that today just flips to "Done" after a single log. Every idea makes it a live daily signal: log at least once a day, re-log anytime, capped once per 6h. Each is shown in its two real states — Unlocked (tap to log) and Cooling down (a live countdown to the next check-in) — at the widget's true footprint next to Nutrition.

Where it lives

Top of Today, paired with Nutrition

Two square widgets side by side. The Feel widget must stay glanceable at ~165 pt and read its state in a single look — that constraint drives all five. Deeper history + notes stay on the Recovery screen.

Same mechanic under all five. A tap writes a check-in row exactly like today's quick picker (energy = sleep = soreness = mood = the picked 1–5).
